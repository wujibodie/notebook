版籍志上
- Intro: 版籍 was introduced by the Qin and Han. "版籍立而治道於是舉矣。"
- 戶口 starts with Song; data missing for Yuan. Ming records: At Hongwu 24, Yongchun's population was 2/3rds of what it had been in the late Song. It continued to drop in the Yongle and Zhengtong censuses, which Lin takes as evidence of a real decline. Since Zhengtong, population has been roughly constant. 
	- Hongwu 24 census: 4041 戶, 21,017 口. 
	- Yongle 10: 2093 戶，6670 口 (ludicrous drop of 14,407, or 2/3s, in 30ish years)
	- Zhengtong 7: 1857 戶, 6907 口. Following censuses stay roughly constant.
	- Jiajing 1: 2017 戶, 6376 口. 
- 土田
	- In the Song, land in Fujian was divided into three grades by 王廷鈞, with the top grade given to Daoist and Buddhist monasteries. But no figures are available for Yongchun in the Song. 
	- Ming: Yongchun's land is divided into 民田, which is all legally the same, and 官田, which has several varieties: 官 (long-standing government land)，職 (land earmarked for salaries)，沒官 (land seized by the state, e.g. from executed criminals)，廢寺 (only example given is Song precedent, the closing of 大理寺)，府學，縣學。
		- Hongwu 24: The total registered land in Yongchun was 2437 qing, 80 mu. Drops to 1631 qing in Yongle 10, then constant every census thereafter -- literally changing by just tens of mu. 
		- In Jiajing 1, of 1631 qing: *tian*: 78 qing 官, 2 職，26 沒, 2 廢寺, 13 府學, 58 縣學, 1488 民. 地：58 畝 官， 13.02 頃 民. 山: 6.64 頃 官，13.02 頃 民. 
		- Lin's commentary: Explains drop in registered land as consequence of population decrease, as land was left fallow. Mentions that this perception of Yongchun having empty land led the court to establish tuntian there, only to cause strife and lawsuits with the locals (pretty clear admission that the land wasn't really empty...)
- 稅糧
	- In intro to Ming section, notes that one category of tax, the 秋租糧, was only levied on former temple lands and school fields. (NB: these would be lands where the state took over as "landlord" for preexisting tenants, so collected the former rent as tax; hence 租.)
		- This tax was quite high: 240 文 per 畝 on 廢寺田, 三貫450文（!) for 學田，while 夏稅鈔 was 3 文/畝 for most other land categories. Reflection that lands liable for autumn rent were more explicitly conceived as revenue raisers? (In case of 學田, this makes sense as rents were earmarked for schools.)
		- Numbers given for Jiajing 1 have 廢寺地 paying 1 釘 856文 in autumn rent, 縣學地 paying 3貫410文, and 廢寺山 paying 3錠2貫202文. Commuted to silver this represents 13 石 2 斗, equivalent to half the guantian summer tax in Yongchun. (Though note that Mintian summer tax dwarfs both, at 119 錠.)
	- Notes that by this time the 夏稅 has been commuted to silver altogether, at a rate of 2分5釐 per 1 錠 of 鈔 quota, while the 秋租糧 is collected in rice. The total 秋租 rice is c. 1% of the 官田 秋糧, and c. .2% of the 民田 autumn tax levy. So not too significant at that scale, but the manner of its use may have been important. 
		- The 秋糧 rice tax was partially converted to silver itself at this time, at a rate of 3-4 分 per 斗. First suggested by 沈御使 （沈灼 ). Current practice is to commute half of 民米 for transport to capital and collect the other half in kind for local storage. In Jiajing 1 commuted 官米 = 450 兩, 3 錢, while 民米 = 140 兩，4 錢. Remaining half of 民米 = 4053 石. 
			- Confused by these numbers, namely how 官米 could convert to higher quantity of silver than 民米; did the guanmi conversion rate differ, or was official land much more heavily taxed in autumn while mintian mostly payed summer tax?
- 版籍下
	- 物料: note that most goods are commuted to silver in Chenghua, Hongzhi, or Zhengde years. Major categories are 弓箭 (175 liang), 軍器 (40 liang), 白糖 (88 liang). 
	- 徭役: Notes that early disputes about the Junyao were largely resolved by allowing the hiring of substitutes, but in Yongchun and Dehua one stills finds disputants in front of the yamen claiming to have been ruined (破產) by junyao levies. 
		- Concludes with a reflection on the fiscal debates of the northern Song between monetary taxation and corvee levies after the Xining reforms (which, typically, Lin takes as reaction against monastic exemption from labor service); advocates a middle path between Wang Anshi and Sima Guang.
		- “朝役法不專於差，不專於僱，因而用之存乎，變利而應之.”
	- Final comment is on economic/fiscal differences between northwest and southeast: “論曰：今之徭役，西北出於丁，東南兼論田。西北之民，田愈多則累愈重，故役不可以論田而論丁。東南之民，以田爲貧富之差，故兼丁田而論之.”
