- Ye Chunji was from Huizhou in Guangdong. States his first task on appointment to Huian was to gather some 30 reputable locals for a banquet to interview them on basic facts; this became the **圖告問**. Wrote the **版籍考** in the course of taxation and accounting duties. **4**
- Habitations & communities are described in the **地理表**. Population listed in **戶口表**. Points out that the excessive levies associated with registration have led people to avoid it + made the numbers an undercount by at least 2/3rds — understandably, **奈何罪民哉?** **5**
- His **田土表** addresses serious issues with the Ming system: private trade in land allows hoarding and inequality, while the garrisons (designed on model of western (desert) frontiers(!)) occupy fertile land while the soldiers starve, doubly burdening the state. Salt production is also insufficient.
- His **賦稅表** shows that even non-fertile lands are assessed taxes, so money commutation has long been practiced in Fujian (**銀稅輪輸者易以粟, 自開之故**). Complains that registers for maritime taxes, farmland, and wastage hadn't been updated in 40 years.
- Further introduces **鄉約篇** (principle of self-governance for each of the 41 **都**), **里社篇** (local religious/folk customs), **保甲篇** (concern that **保** members themselves are turning to crime, so needs for tighter formal regulation vs. early Ming community policing), and **社學** (Ye counts 219 schools,  contrasting the 551 altars to spirits/gods). 6
    - Ye aligns himself with the core pillars of his reform movement (a la Geng Dingxiang): **鄉約**, **保甲**, and suppression of cults in favor of schools.
- Ends preface with a note that peoples' **風** comes from local geography, but their **俗** reflects their rulers' **情欲**, and so is malleable. Then finishes with a short dialogue where he defends the reforms in Hui'an as following the intent of Hongwu, like retuning an old instrument. **7**

**Parts 4–8, page 64–326**, consist of detailed maps + the tables giving the **戶**, **官民田**, tax quotas, and so on for each individual **都**. Then the "reformist" chapters start on 328 with the **鄉約**. The (separate) **崇武所城志** starts on 377.
