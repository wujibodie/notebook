**序一 (Preface 1):** At first, Haicheng was where the mountains met the sea, but through vigorous diking it was made to submit as soil (**處者阻師戰波臣而土之**).

- It seems the gazetteer's compilation was requested by "**南海梁公**" while an office in **福京** (Fuzhou).
    
- In the debates over its compilation, some editors were frustrated that no written material survived before the Song; to which Wang replied: "**其難以縣新也; 其美跡亦以其縣新也**" Compares Haicheng to the similarly new Shunde, which has a good recent gazetteer.
    
- Notes that the **寇** (pirate/bandit) problem has improved since Haicheng became a _xian_; indeed this was the only option, as Haicheng’s situation means its people would always ride the waves to survive, so having official protection is crucial.
    

**序 2 (梁兆陽, to the incoming Zhixian 林郎):** Mostly focuses on the generic virtues of a gazetteer, from his perspective as an outsider wishing to place Haicheng in a broader imperial and historical perspective. Notes discussions with other compilers, including **Zhang Xie** (張燮).

**Juan 1** 
建置沿革: Institutional evolution of Haicheng. Basic narrative of how it became a xian: first, in JJ 42, Tan Lun suggested establishing a coastal defense office there 海防同知. Then in 43 the Haidao 周賢 defeated a group of pirates, and then the candidates-in-waiting (聽選官) 李英，陳鑾 requested for the first time that Haicheng be made a county, and the emperor ordered Fujian's officials to deliberate on the matter. In 44 the Zhifu 唐九德 memorialized to the throne on cutting part of Longxi off as a new Haicheng county, and Wang Daokun memorialized in support; the name Haicheng was suggested at this time. The new county was formally established in Longqing 1. 
- NB: The xun'an yushi Wang Xun had proposed to make Yuegang a county as early as JJ 36, during Ruan E's tenure. Notes that during wokou troubles of that and the next year, local elements rebelled and supported the wokou, which persisted even up to Tan Lun placing an official there. 

**Juan 2**

- The city wall was built in Jiajing 36 by **中丞 阮鶚**, along with a bridge, and used to protect the 8th + 9th **都** and 6th **圖** – note that "city wall" precedes Haicheng becoming a county seat. In Longqing 1, when Haicheng xian was established, the 8th + 9th wards were turned into **堡** (**分為二堡**) responsible for expanding their portions of the walls.

- In Longqing 4 the prefect **羅青霄** requested funds (in silver) to hire artisans to tear down and reinforce parts of the earth walls in stone.
    

**334: In Tianqi 壬戌**, the **紅夷** (Red Barbarians/Dutch) arrived at Haicheng with the intention of landing, and the authorities were unsure what to do, decide to repair and expand the fortifications towards the sea. The secondary, inland fortification managed by the 9th ward is replaced with stone at this time.

- In the **丁卯** year, "**鄭賊入寇**" (Zheng bandits/pirates invade), but the two forts working in unison are able to repel them, thanks to the foresight of the men who built up their walls.
