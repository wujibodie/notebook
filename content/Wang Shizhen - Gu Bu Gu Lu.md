- Opening section: Confucius once said: "Is a goblet not a goblet? Oh goblet! Oh goblet!" He must have been lamenting that [his time's] goblets were not used as old goblets were. This is what is called "slicing off corners to make a circle," or "hacking at the simple to make it ornate." He also said: "I recall when historians left blanks [when in doubt], and when people lent each other their horses to ride; now that's all lost!" He wrote the Spring and Autumn Annals, and he gave away his left chariot horse for funeral expenses; I believe everything contained hidden directions. I was 15 when I first learned a little of human affairs; then I put on the cap and entered the court, and went through many stumbles and rises, up until I returned to my farm; now I'm nearing 60. Tall banks form a valley, and the river follows them down. How many can distinguish when a goblet has ceased being a goblet? In my idleness I have occasionally considered [such cases] and written them out. The largest [involve] court regulations, the smaller village customs, and they even reach down to the minuteness of a single vessel or object; none of this is not worth sighing over. Of those things which are seen as correct now but were rejected in the past, those non-goblet goblets, there are no more than two or three cases in a hundred. When my writing was done, I looked at it and named it the "A Goblet Is Not A Goblet Records".
- Complains that titles have become more complex since the Jiajing reign, particularly the title "weng" suffering inflation: 正德中，稱謂尤簡。至嘉靖中，始有稱翁者，然不過施之於三品九卿耳。其後四五品京堂翰林，以至方伯憲長，皆稱翁矣。今則翰林科道吏部，以至大參僉憲郡守，無不稱翁矣。又其甚者，部屬在外，及丞倅司理，亦稱翁矣。此其諂諛阘冗，流穢人目，固無足道。而又有一種可怪者，往時於鱗與余頗厭惡之。與子與輩尺牘，相聞以字，然不過知已十余人。至於詩文，稱字稍廣，然亦僅施之年位輩行相若者耳。今貧士書生，不見錄有司，輸粟者富家兒，不識一丁，口尚乳鼻，輒戴紫陽巾，衣忠靜衣，挾行卷詩題尺牘，俱稱於鱗伯玉，而究之尚未識面。
- Complains that around the time of the wokou troubles, xunfus started having their arrivals announced by music, which previously had been reserved for zongbing, and the practice moved down the hierarchy from there: 先朝之制，惟總兵官列營，始舉炮奏鼓吮。而吾蘇韓襄毅公雍，以右都御史總督兩廣開府梧州最盛。自是三邊宣大之總督，以至內地帶提督者皆然。若巡撫則不爾。先君代楊襄毅總督駐密雲，晚堂則不舉炮奏鼓吹，云楊公固如是，得非密雲邇京輦，當稍從裁者耶。然自是之後，巡撫亦無不舉炮奏鼓吹，倭變來，巡江御史亦行之。五六年前，吾州兵道亦行之。內地之人，少聞金鼓，無不駭異。又每一臺使行部，則寂然無聲，去而復作，殊不為雅。

- Thoughts on art: in painting one should esteem the Song, but people have lately appreciated the Yuan and even Ming painters up to Shen Zhou. In porcelain, Xuande, and even Yongle and Chenghua pottery has become prized. This trend originated in Wu and then spread via Huizhou people. It's even come to pass that the work of specific contemporary artisans command double the ordinary prices (though less than the 10x markup on antiques), such that those artisans "sit with the gentry," and they are even appreciated in the palace, suggesting the trend will go on. 畫當重宋。而三十年來忽重元人，乃至倪元鎮以逮明沈周，價驟增十倍。窯器當重哥汝。而十五年來忽重宣德，以至永樂成化，價亦驟增十倍。大抵吳人濫觴，而徽人導之，俱可怪也。今吾吳中陸子剛之治玉，鮑天成之治犀，朱碧山之治銀，趙良璧之治錫，馬勛治扇，周治治商嵌，及歙呂愛山治金，王小溪治瑪瑙，蔣抱雲治銅，皆比常價再倍。而其人至有與縉紳坐者，近聞此好流入宮掖，其勢尚未已也。
