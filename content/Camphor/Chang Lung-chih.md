- “As outlined by Shen Baozhen and Liu Mingchuan, the kaishan fufan campaign aimed to amend the discrepancies engendered by obsolete quarantine practices and the aboriginal boundary policy that separated mountain aborigines from the Han Chinese and plains aborigine communities.
