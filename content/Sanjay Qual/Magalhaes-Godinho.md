The debit of European mines has fallen to 21,000 kg; but the Spanish galleons and flotillas leaving from Mexico and Peru take 130,000 kg annually. Despite such a swelling, the drainage towards the Orient turns out to be more virulent than in the previous half-century. And the new route from Acapulco to Manila opens a puncture in the American production: one million pesos per year." 335
