# Lin Xiyuan (林希元)

## Biographical Summary
林希元 （次崖）- 泉州同安人. Jinshi ZD 12. Most famous in his lifetime for his Yijing textbook 易經存疑. Involved closely in overseas commerce after retiring home and finding himself in genteel poverty. Among the Fujian gentry accused by Zhu Wan of cooperating with smugglers. Close associate and patron of Yu Dayou.
- 同安林次崖先生文集， Toyo Bunko V-5-C-235
- Four volumes of his writings are in the Ming Jingshi Wenbian.
