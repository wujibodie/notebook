# Hu Zongxian (胡宗憲)

## Biographical Summary
胡宗憲 (梅林) - 安徽績溪人. Jinshi JJ 17. 兵部右侍郎，總督浙直福建軍務. Supreme Commander of the coordinated anti-wokou campaigns in Zhejiang during the 1550s-60s, overseeing Qi Jiguang and Yu Dayou's professional armies. Established the model of silverized mercenary forces (客兵) that would become central to Fujian's military structure.
