# Geng Dingxiang (耿定向)

## Biographical Summary
耿定向 - 湖北黃安人. Jinshi JJ 35. Supporter of Zhang Juzheng. Hosted Li Zhi before falling out with him. Xunfu of Fujian from Wanli 6 to Wanli 8. Huang Zongxi placed him in the Taizhou School (bio on 355). Zhang Juzheng referred to him as a friend among the Hanlin scholars (答列鄉毛介川).
