# Pang Shangpeng (龐尚鵬)

## Biographical Summary
龐尚鵬 (少南，惺菴) - 廣東南海人. JJ 32 jinshi. Xunfu of Fujian from Wanli 4 to Wanli 6. First letter from Zhang was received while still charged with the salt monopoly; Zhang approved of a memorial 鹽政二十事，屯政便宜數十事. Pang was removed from salt post despite Zhang's support. Later appointed Xunfu of Fujian with mandate to implement the Single Whip, but was removed after sending Zhang a letter urging him to go into mourning.
- His 名山藏 bio: 庞尚鹏，字少南，南海人。嘉靖三十一年进士，授乐平知县。表树风声，政教大行。召入为御史，命核浙直军饷，发总制胡宗宪、监军鄢懋卿破之。奸语侵相，几中奇祸。
