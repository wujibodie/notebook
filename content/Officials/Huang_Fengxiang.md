# Huang Fengxiang (黃鳳翔)

## Biographical Summary
黃鳳翔 - 福建泉州晉江人. Jinshi LQ 2. 南京禮部尚書. Most successful Minnan official of his day. Wrote a stele inscription on the Anhai 駐鎮. Opposed Zhang Juzheng's repressions of critical officials in the duoqing controversy. Edited an edition of the Quanzhou Fuzhi.
- Wrote a 嘉靖政宜.
- His preface to the Quanzhou Gazetteer begins on p. 23 of my pdf version.
