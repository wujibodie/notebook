# Zhu Liangbao (朱良寶)

## Biographical Summary
朱良寶 - Pirate fleet commander identified alongside Lin Feng as an immediate threat in Liu Yaohui's first comprehensive memorial (autumn 1573). Operating across the Guangdong border. Represents the broader pirate networks that Fujian authorities had to contend with beyond the single figure of Lin Feng.
