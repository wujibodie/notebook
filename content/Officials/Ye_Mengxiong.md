# Ye Mengxiong (葉夢熊)

## Biographical Summary
葉夢熊 - 广东归善人. Jinshi JJ 44. 兵部尚书、右佥都御史巡抚甘肃. Participated in suppressing the Bo Cheng rebellion in Ningxia. Served as Zhixian of Fuqing early in his career.
