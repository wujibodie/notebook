# Tan Lun (譚綸)

## Biographical Summary
譚綸 （二華, 子理，襄敏) - 江西宜黃人. Jinshi JJ 23. Xunfu of Fujian in JJ 42-43. Started his anti-wokou career as Taizhou Zhifu in JJ 29. Credited with Qi Jiguang and Yu Dayou for defeating the wokou in Fujian. In his time as Xunfu of Fujian, implemented the 四六分法 seizing 6/10ths of monastic land as 官田. Transferred to northern border in Longqing 2-4. Frequent correspondent of Zhang's, especially early in ZJZ's career.
