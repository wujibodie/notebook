# Xu Fuyuan (許孚遠)

## Biographical Summary
許孚遠 - 浙江德清人. Jinshi JJ 41. Xunfu of Fujian, Wanli 20-23. Early in his career was supported by Gao Gong, and appointed 按察佥事 in Guangdong, then moved to Fujian after victories against pirates. His collected writings include correspondence with Zhang Juzheng. Received the delegation from Manila on Dasmariñas' murder.
- Wenji: 敬和堂集
