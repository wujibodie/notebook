# Wang Daokun (汪道昆)

## Biographical Summary
汪道昆 （玉卿, 南明/溟, 伯玉）- 直隸歙縣人. 福建巡撫. Jinshi JJ 26 (same as Zhang Juzheng and Wang Shizhen). As 福州兵備道, held civil oversight over Qi and Yu's campaigns against the Wo (~1563-4). Close associate of Tan Lun. Replaced Tan as Xunfu of Fujian from 1565-7, but was removed just before LQ1. Under Zhang Juzheng, served as 兵部右，左侍郎 while Tan was Bingbu Shangshu. Also a noted poet and associate of Wang Shizhen's literary group.
