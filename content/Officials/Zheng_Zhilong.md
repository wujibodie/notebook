# Zheng Zhilong (鄭芝龍)

## Biographical Summary
鄭芝龍 (一官，飛黃) - 福建泉州南安人. The great maritime lord of the late Ming/Ming-Qing transition. Not strictly within the 16th century timeframe, but essential for understanding where the structures analyzed in the dissertation ultimately led. He Qiaoyuan was among his ardent supporters in the Quanzhou gentry. Represents the culmination of the privatized military-commercial networks that figures like Yu Dayou pioneered and Liu Yaohui struggled to reform.
