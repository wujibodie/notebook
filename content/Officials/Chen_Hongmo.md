# Chen Hongmo (陳洪謨)

## Biographical Summary
陳洪謨 - 湖廣武陵人. Jinshi ZD 9. 漳州知府 during the Zhengde era. Mentioned in Zhangzhou gazetteers in connection with educational and religious institutions and the development of the prefecture. Represents early 16th century Zhangzhou administration before the wokou crisis.
