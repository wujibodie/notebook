# Zeng Yiben (曾一本)

## Biographical Summary
曾一本 - Guangdong pirate/bandit active during Longqing-Wanli transition. Raided Guangzhou and coastal areas; the treasonous general Zhou Yunxiang killed the Leiqiong 參將 Geng Zongyuan and joined Zeng's forces. Zhang Han was punished for failing to suppress him as Liangguang Zongdu. His campaigns illustrate the inter-provincial piracy problem that bedeviled Ming coastal defense.
