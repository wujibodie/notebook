# Zhang Yue (張岳)

## Biographical Summary
張岳 （(諡)襄惠）- 福建泉州惠安人. Jinshi ZD 12. Jiangxi, Liangguang Xunfu, Bingbu Shangshu. Muzhiming by Xu Jie. Only Hui'an native to have a Mingshi liezhuan. While in Liangguang, oversaw diplomatic campaigns in response to the Mac-Le conflict in Annam; rejected the more militaristic policy of Lin Xiyuan, for which Lin was then stripped of office.
- Edited the Jiajign era 惠安縣志. Also co-edited the 嘉靖廣東通志初稿 together with Dai Jing (!!!).
