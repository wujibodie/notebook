# Liu Ji (劉畿)

## Biographical Summary
劉畿 （子京，羽泉) - 直隸蘇州府長洲縣人. Jinshi JJ 29. Zhejiang Xunfu, 總督浙直江西
. Operated in Zhejiang from JJ 43 - 5, d. LQ 1; coordinated closely with Pan Jixun and Pang Shangpeng (Xun'an Yushi at the same time). Involved in fiscal retrenchment after the peak of wokou crisis had passed, in cooperation with Single Whip reformers, but also helped coordinate massive trans-provincial campaigns against Wu Ping.
