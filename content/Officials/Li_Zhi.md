# Li Zhi (李贄)

## Biographical Summary
李贄 (卓吾，宏甫) - 福建泉州晉江人. Jinshi JJ 31. 姚安知府. The most famous heterodox thinker from Minnan, known for his 焚書 and engagement with commerce, Buddhism, and moral philosophy. Returned to Quanzhou in 1560 after his father's death and describes defending city walls from wokou. Hosted by Geng Dingxiang at Huang'an before their famous public falling-out. His writings provide important first-person accounts of wokou-era Quanzhou and represent a distinctive intellectual tradition counter to the Cheng-Zhu ort
