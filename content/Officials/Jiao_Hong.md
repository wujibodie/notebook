# Jiao Hong (焦竑)

## Biographical Summary
焦竑 (弱候，澹園）- 南京旗手衛人. Zhuangyuan in WL 17. Mostly held Hanlin and examiner appointments, with a brief banishment as 福寧州同知. Student of Geng Dingxiang and associate of Li Zhi's. Author of a number of historical works, including the biographical encyclopedia 國朝獻徵錄.
