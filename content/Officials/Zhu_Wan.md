# Zhu Wan (朱紈)

## Biographical Summary
朱紈 (子純) - 浙江長興人. Jinshi ZD 9. 浙江巡撫，提督浙閩海防軍務. The zealous anti-piracy official of the Jiajing reign who prosecuted Fujian gentry accused of cooperating with smugglers, including members of Lin Xiyuan's circle. His aggressive enforcement of the maritime ban created powerful enemies among coastal elites; he was eventually impeached, stripped of office, and committed suicide (JJ 29).
