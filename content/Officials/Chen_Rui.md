# Chen Rui (陈瑞)

## Biographical Summary
陈瑞 - 福建长乐人. Jinshi JJ 32. 两广总督，湖廣巡撫（萬曆３－６), 兵部尚书. Involved in relief efforts for flood victims in Gong'an county and suppression of bandits in Guangdong. Eventually befriended and supportsed Matteo Ricci. Impeached in 1583 as part of anti-Zhang purges.
