# Ling Yunyi (凌雲翼)

## Biographical Summary
凌雲翼 (洋山) - 南直隸太倉州人. Jinshi JJ 26. 兵部尚書，兩廣總督. Zongdu of Liangguang during the latter phase of the Lin Feng campaigns (succeeding Yin Zhengmao). His Mingshi biography provides key details about Hu Shouren's pursuit of Lin Feng and Wang Wanggao's embassy to Luzon. Successfully completed the pacification of Luopang. Known for aggressive military tactics but also criticized for excessive violence.
