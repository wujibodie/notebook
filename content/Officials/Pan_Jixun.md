# Pan Jixun (潘季驯)

## Biographical Summary
潘季驯 （印川） - 浙江 湖州 乌程人. JJ 29 jinshi. Pushed the 均平法 in Guangdong in the early 1560s. From 1565-1592 repeatedly served as 河道总领, and was respected as the foremost hydraulic expert of his age.
