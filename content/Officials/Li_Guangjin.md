# Li Guangjin (李光縉)

## Biographical Summary
李光縉 - 福建泉州晉江人. Quanzhou literatus, author of 景璧集. Provides important accounts of Anping merchants, including the biography of Li Yuxi (who escaped the 1603 Manila massacre because he anticipated the eunuch-driven disaster) and the porcelain merchant 狀天夔. His writings are key sources for understanding Minnan merchant life and overseas trade networks.
