# He Qiaoyuan (何喬遠)

## Biographical Summary
何喬遠 (稚孝，匪蓮) - 福建泉州晉江人. Jinshi WL 14. 禮部右侍郎. Quanzhou literatus whose writings (鏡山全集, 閩書, 海上小議, 開洋海議) provide crucial accounts of Minnan maritime trade, the wokou crisis, and relations with overseas Chinese communities. An ardent supporter of Zheng Zhilong in the Quanzhou gentry. His correspondence with Zheng Zhilong and advocacy for maritime trade opening make him essential for understanding late Ming Fujian's commercial-official networks.
