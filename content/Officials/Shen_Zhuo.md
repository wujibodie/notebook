# Shen Zhuo (沈灼)

## Biographical Summary
沈灼 - 南直隸嘉定人. Jinshi ZD 3. Guangxi, Zhejiang yushi; First Fujian xun'an yushi, then in Jiajing 2 became 福建按察司僉事. Credited by Gu Yanwu with extending silver commutation of guantian rice tax in Fujian. In the Wanli Quanzhou Fuzhi (版籍志), he is credited with introducing the 八分法 to Quanzhou.
