# Gao Cai (高寀)

## Biographical Summary
高寀 - 宦官 (eunuch). Active in Fujian maritime customs in the late Wanli period. Blamed for provoking the Manila massacre of 1603 through his taxation policies on grain merchants' boats. His interference in maritime affairs exemplifies the complications that followed Liu Yaohui's more measured approach to maritime customs. Connected to Yu Zigao's misadventures in Manila and Penghu.
