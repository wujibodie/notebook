# Lin Feng (林鳳)

## Biographical Summary
林鳳 - 廣東饒平人. The principal pirate antagonist in Liu Yaohui's tenure as Fujian Xunfu. Rose to prominence during the 1560s wokou conflicts; by the early 1570s commanded a substantial fleet operating across Fujian-Guangdong waters. In late 1574, led an expedition of 2,000-4,000 men across 60+ vessels to attack the nascent Spanish settlement at Manila. Repelled by Spanish forces, he retreated to Pangasinan and was besieged; escaped autumn 1575 with ~1,000 followers and returned to Chaozhou to rebuild
