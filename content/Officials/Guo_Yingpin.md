# Guo Yingpin (郭应聘)

## Biographical Summary
郭应聘 - 福建莆田人. Jinshi JJ 29. 兵部尚书、右都御史兼兵部右侍郎总督两广军务. Specialist in Southwestern military affairs. Started in Guangxi under Yin Zhengmao 殷正茂, then served as Guangxi Xunfu under Liu Yaohui during latter's time as Liangguang Zongdu; later served as Zongdu himself. Suppressed rebellions in Guangxi including Gutian bandits and Yao people in Fujiang and Huaiyuan. Per Mingshi bio, was an admirer of Wang Yangming and associate of Hai Rui.
