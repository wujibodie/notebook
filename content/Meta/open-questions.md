## Dissertation — The Coast / Liu Yaohui Chapter

### lin-feng-1573-76-activities
**Question:** What was Lin Feng doing between 1573–74, and what became of him after the failed Manila expedition (1574–75)? Did he return to Fujian coast? Is there any Ming-side documentation of his movements post-1576?
**Why it matters:** Lin Feng is central to the Diagnosing the Crisis chapter; the gap between his documented Fujian raiding and the Manila attack is unaccounted for.
**Where to look:** Tang Kaijian's Lin Feng study (Summaries/Tang_Kaijian_Lin_Feng_Study.md); Spanish primary sources (Colin, Chirino, beyond what's been read); 籌辦夷務始末; Shunde-Chaozhou Fuzhi on Guangdong response.
**Added:** 2026-04-26 | **Status:** open

### liu-yu-conflict-episodes
**Question:** What are the concrete episodes of conflict or friction between Liu Yaohui and Yu Dayou during Liu's tenure (1572–1576)? What form did the tension take — personnel decisions, memorial disputes, tactical disagreements?
**Why it matters:** The bureaucratic vs. charismatic authority contrast is a key analytical frame; needs documentary grounding.
**Where to look:** Liu Yaohui - Xulai Ji (Primary Sources/); Zhang Juzheng's letters to Liu; Yu Dayou's Zhengqi Tang Quanji.
**Added:** 2026-04-26 | **Status:** open

### guangdong-trade-channeling-intent
**Question:** Is there positive evidence that Guangdong officials *consciously* funneled maritime trade toward certain ports (vs. simply having a different military strategy from Fujian officials)?
**Why it matters:** Dissertation argument requires distinguishing deliberate fiscal strategy from incidental outcomes.
**Where to look:** Shunde-Chaozhou Fuzhi; Guangdong memorials in Ming Shilu; compare with Fujian-side memorials on same period.
**Added:** 2026-04-26 | **Status:** open

### spanish-primary-sources
**Question:** What Spanish primary sources beyond Chirino and Colin are accessible? Specifically: Manila-based accounts of Lin Feng's 1574 attack; Alcalde's reports; Legazpi correspondence.
**Why it matters:** The Manila expedition is a major episode; current documentation is thin on the Spanish side.
**Where to look:** Archivo General de Indias (Philippines section); Juan Gil, *Los Chinos en Manila* (root-level file in vault).
**Added:** 2026-04-26 | **Status:** open
