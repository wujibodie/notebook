Catiline War
“Sed multi mortales dediti ventri atque somno indocti incultique vitam sicuti peregrinantes transiere;**1** quibus profecto contra naturam corpus voluptati, anima oneri fuit. Eorum ego vitam mortemque iuxta aestumo, quoniam de utraque siletur.” Yet many men, being slaves to appetite and sleep, have passed through life untaught and untrained, like mere wayfarers in these men we see, contrary to Nature's intent, the body a source of pleasure, the soul a burden. For my own part, I consider the lives and deaths of such men as about alike, since no record is made of either.

“Vastus animus immoderata, incredibilia, nimis alta semper cupiebat.”
