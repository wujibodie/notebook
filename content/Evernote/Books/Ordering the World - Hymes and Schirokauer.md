茶馬司 and 常平倉 - Paul Jakov Smith

* Two competing processes in the Northern Song: a long term state retreat from an economy which was growing beyond the reach of a premodern bureaucracy, and a three-century retrenchment of central economic activism beginning in the middle Tang and culminating in Wang Anshi. Smith supports the idea that the latter cycle represented “a final attempt by a uniquely professionalized bureaucratic elite to keep the burgeoning economy under the control of the state.” 77
* Commercial revenues grew both absolutely and as a proportion of total state income throughout the Northern Song, reached about 50% of total government income by 1076. This high level of dependence on the private commercial economy was required by the military threat of the steppe states, and defense expenditure ate up the vast majority of these revenues, in some case forcing over exploitation that led to state withdrawal from certain sectors. 79-81
	* “Beginning in the late Tang, then, the rise of a professional financial service converged with the growing costs of geopolitical insecurity to produce an activist orientation to economic statecraft.” 81
* Wang Anshi: 政事所以理財，理財乃所謂義也。83
