* Example of 陳正祥: in 1983 he used 3000 LGs to compile maps of historical locust disasters in north China. Going by hand, it took him 8 months; Chen estimates that with LogArt this could be done in just 1-2 months. 
* Methodology: 1. Data collection, 2. obtain datasets, 3. Identify patterns through statistical/visual analytics, 4. Return to the whole to refine conclusions.
