Sociality as formal prerequisite for “mind”: 
"Only in virtue of the multilayered semantic structure of language does sociality become a normative space of recognitive-cognitive rational agents; **and the suppos­edly ‘private’ experiences and thoughts of participating agents are only structured** _**as**_ **experiences and thoughts in so far as they are bound up in this normative—-at once intersubjective and objective—spac**e... Perception is only perception because it is apperception, and appercep­tion is only apperceptive in that it is an artefact of a deprivatized semantic space within which recognitive-cognitive agents emerge as by-products of a deeply impersonal space which they themselves have formally conditioned.” 1
