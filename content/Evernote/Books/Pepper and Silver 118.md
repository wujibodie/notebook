Freedman

* "Demand-side" look at pepper imports into Europe. Focus on fashion and signalling value of spices.

Subrahmanyam

* Follows Aubin's interpretation that the Portuguese did not substantially affect the movement of pepper into the Levant, and that the apparent break in imports via Egypt and Venice was due to unrest in the Hejaz before the Mamluk fleet settled matters in 1507-1509.

Finlay

* Comparison between "optimists" and "pessimists" in Venice following Portuguese discovery of India route. Relationship between this mindsets and ongoing political struggles in Italy and Greece

Prange

* Attempt to explain the spice trade by looking at the internal organization of pepper production in the Malabar region, and demand from India, the Middle East and China.
