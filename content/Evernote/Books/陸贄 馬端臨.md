陸贄
1
Three ancient forms of taxation: 租 is the land tax which evolved after the breakdown of the 井田 system. 調 is a tax in silk, payable in other fibers where silk doesn’t grow. 庸 is the corvée service; at first it was set at 3 days per adult male per year, then during the warring states increases to 30 days, before being reduced to 20 days by Tang times; it is commutable by payment in thread.

* 「此三道者，皆宗本前哲之規模，參考曆代之利害，其取法也遠，其立意也深，其斂財也均，其域人也固，其裁規也簡，其備慮也周。」These three taxation systems are the products of long experience and deep consideration, and are well-balanced as a result.
* however, since the chaos of the 天寶 reign, the government has sought to recover state finances by searching through old records for new forms of taxation; to Lu Zhi this is harming the people and causing them to lose faith in the government. 「若好革而不知原始要終，斯皆以弊易弊者也。」Excoriates officials of his time for focusing on 財 rather than 人.「夫作法裕於人，未有不得人者也；作法裕於財，未有不失人者也。」「此乃采非法之權令，以為經制；總無名之暴賦，以立恆規。是務取財，豈雲恤隱？」
* Because wealth is produced by labor, the government must not push people in a way that disincentivizes work. 「是以先王之制賦入也，必以丁夫為本，無求於力分之外，無貸於力分之內。」

Point to six problems with the tax system which particularly increase the people’s weariness:

* During past reigns, powers of taxation were slowly devolved to provincial magistrates, who falsified their records and levied corvée arbitrarily for their own ends. Replacing the variety of local taxes with a single empire-wide system would regularize finances and help the people. Unsure where this comes in: 增損既由郡邑，消息易協物宜，故法雖久刓，而人未甚瘁.
* To finance military costs (During 安史之亂？) the state debased the coinage, adding 200 to the face value of a guan and possibly removing copper. (旋屬征討，國用不充，複以供軍為名，每貫加徵二百，當道或增戎旅，又許量事取資，詔敕皆謂權宜，悉令事畢停罷。) Despite this, taxes were denominated at the old rates. With the former wars over, taxation should return to the real values of the past to decrease the burden on the people.
* Taxes denominated in coin are often paid in bolts of silk. \[I.e., common households must sell cloth to earn their tax coin?\] However, whereas one 匹 used to be valued at roughly 3200 文, the same amount is now worth only 1500 文. This means taxes have effectively doubled while staying at the same nominal amount. \[Why has the price of silk dropped despite the debasement of the currency mentioned in 2? Richard: Lack of coin relative to demand causes widespread deflation; price of all goods go down.)
* When tax payments are transported to the capital, corrupt officials skim off the most valuable portions for themselves and lie about the quantities they received.
* Local officials have found ways to expedite the census taking procedures by taking headcounts of people forced to gather in public spaces; but this is both burdensome and inaccurate. (Actually talking about a profusion of extraordinary taxes added on to the twice a year tax, for example forcing localities to pay administrative fees on an ad how basis, and requisitions of goods for palace use.)
* Now that the two annual taxes have been enacted, they only add to all of the above exactions.
	

2
Lu goes into further detail about the 兩稅 system. Explains that the only convenient sources of taxation are those produced by human labor in the earth; that is, grain and cloth. In the past these were collected directly, but since the 兩稅 reform taxes have been measured by monetary value. This means that when cloth was expensive, only half as much was needed for the same tax payment as in recent times when cloth has become cheap. The effective labor required for a household to make a cash-denominated payment has thus doubled. Lu Zhi recommends switching back to taxation in kind, because it will allow people to focus their efforts on producing their assigned tax goods and prevent the vicissitudes of supply and demand from affecting people’s ability to pay taxes. 是以家給而國足，事均而法行，此直稍循令典之舊規，固非創制之可疑者也。
To the charge that collecting taxes in kind will make it difficult to respond to fluctuations in state needs, Lu answers that of the 3 major expenditures, clothing and food for the army are both covered by in-kind taxes, while official salaries can easily be paid in grain rather than money. This would also allow the state to use money opportunistically rather than being forced to constantly emit coins: 以權為常，則輕重之柄不得專於國。故先王制祿以食，而平貨以錢，然後國有權而家有節矣. Moreover, this is the only way to fill the empty military granaries; the methods of antiquity would not be sufficient. Wrt money, the state must maintain control over minting and ensure a consistent price level. 物賤由乎錢少，少則重，重則加鑄而散之使輕；物貴由乎錢多，多則輕，輕則作法而斂之使重。是乃物之貴賤，係於錢之多少；錢之多少，在於官之盈縮。If the state loses control of the money supply, and tries to win it back via taxes in coin, the poor will be destroyed while hoarders will take control of the practical 權 of prices.

* Suggests expanding bronze mining and severely prohibiting the use of bronze for toolmaking in order to increase the money supply available to the court.
* Monopolies on salt and alcohol (!) are additional means of maintaining cash revenue.
* Seems to claim that monetary taxes have led to a moral decline with economic consequences: 蓋以事逐情生，費從事廣，物有劑而用無節，夫安得不乏乎！The ultimate problem with state finances is a failure to regulate consumption/desire (stimulated by monetary economy), more so than an objective lack of resources.
* Ends by comparing three rulers who started with their lands in poverty but returned them to prosperity via moderation (魯哀公，漢文帝，唐太宗) with three who began with plenty but exhausted the people through excess (秦始皇，漢武帝，隋煬帝). Han Wudi is a partial exception, as he was able to save his dynasty by realizing his mistake and reversing course partway through his reign; implicit comparison to choice confronting current emperor.

Bonus: 資治通鑑

* Lu Zhi memorialized the throne protesting trade between Annam and Guangzhou: “嶺南節度使奏：「近日海舶珍異，多就安南市易，欲遣判官就安南收市，乞命中使一人與俱。」上欲從之，陸贄上言，以為：「遠國商販，惟利是求，緩之斯來，擾之則去。廣州素為眾舶所湊，今忽改就安南，若非侵刻過深，則必招攜失所，曾不內訟，更蕩上心。況嶺南、安南，莫非王土，中使、外使，悉是王臣，豈必信嶺南而絕安南，重中使以輕外使。所奏望寢不行。」”

馬端臨

「按：以錢輸稅而不以榖帛，以資力定稅而不問身丁，人皆以為行兩稅以後之弊，今觀此，則由來久矣。」

東萊呂氏：In the three dynasties, 所謂土貢未必能當貢賦之半，留之於諸侯之國，以待王室之用，皆是三代經常之法。

田畝之在人者，不能禁其賣易，官授田之法盡廢，則向之所謂輸庸、調者，多無田之人矣。乃欲按籍而徵之，令其與豪富兼並者一例出賦可乎？又況遭安、史之亂，丁口流離轉徙，版籍徒有空文，豈堪按以為額？蓋當大亂之後，人口死徙虛耗，豈復承平之舊？其不可轉移失陷者，獨田畝耳。

然則視大歷十四年墾田之數以定兩稅之法，雖非經國之遠圖，乃救弊之良法也。但立法之初，不任土所宜，輸其所有，乃計綾帛而輸錢。既而物價愈下，所納愈多，遂至輸一者過二，重為民困。此乃掊刻之吏所為，非法之不善也。陸宣公與齊抗所言固為切當，然必欲復租、庸、調之法，必先復口分、世業之法，均天下之田，使貧富等而後可，若不能均田，則兩稅乃不可易之法矣。

\[On Lu Zhi’s passage regarding the need for tax policy not to disincentivized hard work\] 此雖名言，然物之不齊，物之情也。均是人也，而才藝有智愚之不同；均營生也，而時運有屯亨之或異。蓋有起窮約而能自致千金，其餘力且足以及他人者；亦有蒙故業而不能保一簪，一身猶以為累者，雖聖人不能比而同之也。然則以田定賦，以家之厚薄為科斂之輕重，雖非盛世事，而救時之策不容不然，未宜遽非也。
