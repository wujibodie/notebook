* Gives five reasons for the slow development of Xiamen’s urban structure in the early 1920s:
	* 1\. The political situation under Zang Zhiping made other matters more urgent than urban development;
	* 2\. The amount of capital required for just the widening and paving of streets ran into the millions of yuan, which the government was not able to raise from any of its principal revenue streams (local taxes, bonds and donations, land sales, harbor fees, etc.), most of which were requisitioned by Zang’s forces;
	* 3\.
