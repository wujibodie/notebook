Antiquity: 民與君為一; although later scholars start their study of antiquity’s institutions with land tenure, Ye sees the giving of land as only one part of a general program to give the people everything they lacked, while asking much from them in terms of labor and taxes. This is what is really meant by the ruler and people being as one body. 

Later: 民與君為二；the rulers let the people do what they are naturally able to, and then take from that stock. In other words, they allow private enterprise to satisfy most needs, then congratulate themselves on occasionally taking some portion in taxes or remitting some resources as aid. Now there are three groups of people (rulers, soldiers, and laborers), each of which is riven by private distinctions. 

* People of this time say the ways of antiquity are distant and cannot be practiced; but they don’t see how much more effort and complication ends up being involved in managing production on private lands.
* Specifically cites vagrancy and underproduction (land left fallow) as consequences of private land tenure; these require ministers to actively intervene, harassing the people with notices and regulations. 

民事中

Population is the foundation of productivity and military strength. Shang Yang invited tillers from the three Jin to strengthen Qin; Sun Quan annexed the 山越 and captured 島居之夷, while Zhuge Liang annexed the south and expanded the family rolls of Hanzhong. Even so, when Wei conquered Wu and Shu, it had a population greater than both combined. 

* The empire now has a population 10 times larger than Cao Wei, and flourishes without rivals etc. Thus it ought to be wealthy; but bad management has lead to poverty, inequality, alienation, and weakness. “民雖多而不知用之，直聽其自生自死而已。"

Because the government does not place people on farmland, those who cannot make a living through talent are reduced too servitude; those who are intelligent become merchants and thieves, and survive without settling into families. Thus everyone is forced to worry about making money to buy food on the market even when food is plentiful. 
    Specifically the old lands of Wu and Yue have become overpopulated, and so the price of food is up 3-5x while rent is up 10x from the "past.” This has produced a culture where people seek to trick each other to win wealth rather than focusing on agriculture and virtue. Ye sees the situation as unsustainable and envisions a collapse of this society w/in decades. Solution: forcibly move the population from Liangzhe and Min to less populated regions. 

* Ending: this is the kind of change which will be better for everyone, but would never happen “naturally;” it depends on the interventionist state.

民事上

The jingtian system cannot be brought back without the fengjian system; mobile, non-local officials cannot manage local land taxes. 
“故後世之所以為不如三代者，罪在於不能使天下無貧民耳，不在乎田之必為井不為井也。”
In modern world, the wealthy, not officials, have “養民之權."

Richard: Ye Shi among a group of scholars pushing for land taxes rather than taxes on wealth and consumption. 

* Southern Song has 4 different monetary regions, but most of it relies on paper money called “huizi,” linked to bronze coin. 
* Sichuan：交子 is originally a private system developed by merchants to get around iron coins. 錢引 is government system developed to replace them.
* Southeast: 會子 introduced in Hangzhou in 1160 to raise funds for new war with Jin Dynasty. Emperor Xiaozong attempted to buy back these notes using silver; but this ended up accidentally turning the notes into a circulating currency backed by government bronze and silver! 
* Huainan: 淮會 introduced by government to circulate among soldiers and civilians at military frontier. 
* Hubei: 湖會 same as above, but in Hubei. 

In Southern Song court, the Chen brothers represent a faction arguing to reintroduce the jingtian system in the present.
