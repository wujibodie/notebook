
Bureaucracy 

What were the boundaries of thinking among the Qing officialdom about state involvement in the economy? In which ways did thinking about the state’s responsibilities to society change over the course of the dynasty? In particular, how were tensions between the need to finance the necessary functions of state and the preference for low taxation and light interference in local activities strengthened or resolved over time?

  

Maritime 

To what extent did the Qing exert authority over its maritime frontiers? What was the relationship between central court decision-making and the agency of officials in the maritime provinces with regards to foreign affairs (commercial, diplomatic, or military)? Previous generations of scholarship have highlighted the end of the war with the Zhengs in 1683 and the Treaty of Nanjing in 1842 as key turning points in the overall maritime policy of the Qing — to what extent does more recent scholarship confirm or challenge this periodization? 

  

Enterprise

What were the major institutional forms taken by private enterprise in Qing China before 1840? To what extent did new types of business organizations evolve from or replace these forms in the 80 years to 1920? What role was played by "bureaucratic entrepreneurship" across this timespan?



- As early as the 1950s, Feuerwerker highlighted the consistent influence of venerable organizational forms like the salt gabelle on "modern" industries in the late Qing.
