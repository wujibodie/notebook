    19|    19|    19|# 同安林次崖先生文集·上册
    20|    20|    20|
    21|    21|    21|Lin Xiyuan (1480–1560), zi Maozhen, hao Ciya (次崖), a native of Tong'an county, Quanzhou prefecture, Fujian. Jinshi 1517 (正德十二年丁丑榜). Served as magistrate of Sizhou (泗州), assistant surveillance commissioner of Guangdong, vice minister of the Court of the Imperial Stud (南京大理寺丞), and prefect of Qinzhou (欽州知州). A committed Cheng-Zhu scholar who opposed the Wang Yangming school. His 《四書存疑》 and 《易經存疑》 were standard examination texts alongside Cai Qing's 《蒙引》.
    22|    22|    22|
