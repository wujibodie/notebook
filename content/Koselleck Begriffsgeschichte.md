- Earliest "systematic fusion" of social and conceptual history, which were present as two problematics from the Enlightenment on, occurred with Walter Schlesinger and Otto Brunner in the 30s, who were equivalent to Erich Rothacker, Carl Schmitt, and Jost Trier. In France, the analog was the Annales school, for whom "linguistic analysis was an integral part of their social-historical research," under the rubric "Mots et choses."

- Example of conceptual history of marriage notes that, up to the 18th century, marriage was conceived  theologically-legally as an institution for the propagation of humankind, and thus people incapable of providing for a family could be denied the right to marry. In Prussia this condition was removed in the Landrecht of 1791-94, with the freedom of the consenting partners raised to the higher principle, even to the point of allowing divorce. Thereafter the notion of marriage as contract of "free individuals" gave rise to the idea of "love marriage." 27-8
