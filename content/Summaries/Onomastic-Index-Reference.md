# Onomastic Index: Reference Work Note

## Onomastic Index of Ming Officials

The Onomastic Index serves as a comprehensive reference work documenting biographical information for over 200 officials relevant to Minnan fiscal and military history during the Jiajing-Wanli periods (1522–1620). Rather than constituting a published secondary source, this index functions as a scholarly tool compiled from primary source materials including *Ming Shi* biographies, local gazetteers, collected works (*wenji*), and epigraphic records. It provides standardized information on each official's native place, examination dates, career appointments, and relevant policy positions.

The index is organized alphabetically by surname and includes entries for major figures such as Zhang Juzheng (with details on his reform network), Liu Yaohui (documenting his Fujian governorship and Lin Feng campaigns), Pang Shangpeng (tracing his Zhejiang and Fujian tenures), and Yu Dayou (recording his military career and local networks). Cross-references connect officials through patronage relationships, factional alignments, and policy collaborations—for example, linking Pan Jixun's Guangdong reforms as direct inspiration for Pang Shangpeng's later Zhejiang innovations. Geographic indexing enables rapid identification of all officials connected to specific prefectures or counties.

**Use Note**: When encountering named officials in primary sources, consult this index for biographical context including examination cohorts (relevant for understanding patronage networks), career trajectories (illuminating administrative experience), and documented policy positions (clarifying ideological alignments). The index is particularly valuable for identifying connections between central court politics and provincial implementation—for instance, tracing how Zhang Juzheng's examination relationships influenced appointments to key fiscal posts. However, as a reference compilation rather than analytical scholarship, material from this index should be verified against primary sources and cited appropriately to original documents.
