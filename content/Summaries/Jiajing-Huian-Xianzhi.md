《嘉靖惠安縣誌》(Jiajing Hui'an County Gazetteer)

The Jiajing era Hui'an County Gazetteer contains valuable household registration data from the Jiajing 1st year (嘉靖元年, 1522) yellow registers (黃冊), documenting the demographic composition of this coastal Minnan county at the beginning of the Jiajing reign. The gazetteer's compiler includes a striking commentary noting that military households (軍戶) comprised nearly one-third of all registered households and their adult male population (丁口) approached half of the civilian population. The compiler expresses moral discomfort with the hereditary military registration system that perpetuated punishment across generations (父子兄弟不能相見), noting that although household numbers had declined significantly, the laws against concealment and evasion remained in force. This passage reflects growing awareness among local elites during the mid-Ming of the institutional contradictions between Hongwu-era registration systems and contemporary demographic and economic realities.

The gazetteer is particularly valuable for understanding the military-civilian demographic imbalance in coastal Fujian during the early wokou crisis period. The data shows 1,833 civilian households with 16,201 members versus 1,360 military households with 18,295 members—a remarkable inversion where military households averaged significantly more adult males per household than civilians. This reflects both the reality of military colonization in the region and the burden of hereditary military service that encouraged families to maximize registered males for rotation purposes. The compiler's editorial comment advocating institutional reform (窮則變，變則通) situates this local data within broader debates about Ming fiscal and military institutional reform during the Jiajing era, demonstrating how local gazetteers served as venues for policy criticism and reform advocacy during this period of crisis and transformation.
