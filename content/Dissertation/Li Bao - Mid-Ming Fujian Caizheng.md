
Abstract: 有明一代，“费出于役”是地方财政的基本特征。明中期以来，福建地方行政支出及额外征收日渐增多，所需皆以“里甲役”与“均徭役”的名目向里甲摊派，造成基层里甲疲敝，丧失应有机能；与此同时，中央向地方施加的征税压力不减反增。困境之下，福建地方自发进行如“纲银法”“八分法”等役法改革。役法改革后，福建地方确立了以**官府为中心地位的收支体系**，财政职权大为伸张，形成了行政职能总体虽趋于萎缩，但在财政职权上却呈现局部扩张趋势. 

- Previous scholars have emphasized the shrinking of public functions with the mid-Ming breakdown of the lijia, but have overlooked the expansion of government capacities through the *yifa* 役法. In fact, certain bureaus expanded even as the whole apparatus "shriveled." Fujian stands out as one of the pioneering regions in the *yifa* reforms, with unique reforms like the *gangyin fa* 纲银法. 
- Starts with the example of road- and bridgeworks, which in the early Ming were undertaken by the central administration, but increasingly came to be managed by private individuals and trusts. 
- Although Zheng Zhenman sees the retreat of the provincial authorities from public works as a result of greater oversight of / limits to their demands on the lijia system, Li points out that such works simultaneously were assigned to more specialized offices still under central supervision. 
- Taxation had been seen as one of six major responsibilities of local officials in the early Ming, but Zhang Juzheng considered it the primary duty: "“均徭、赋役、里甲、驿递， 乃有司第一议." 
	- ZTYWJ, 答保定巡抚孙立亭
- Li argues that the *yifa* reforms arose to alleviate the crises caused by government spending outstripping what was sustainable under the "Hongwu-style" fiscal system. Government demands frequently caused hardships for the people, in turn leading to reforms to limit those demands or shift them to more predictable forms --> monetization. 
- By the mid/late 15th century, Fujian gazetteers began to mention "commutation silver treasuries" as a normal part of local government. (Pg. 9, citing 阳思谦:《泉州府志》卷六《赋役》 )
- Geng Dingxiang wrote that as the household registrations were scattered 户籍棼散, the Litu transformed into the Baojia. Citing 《耿天台先生文集》卷一八《牧事末议》

Stages of reform
- 八分法: Enacted in 1520 by Fujian Xun'an yushi Shen Zhuo 沈灼. Unique to Fujian. Involved transmuting grain and labor levies at a rate of 8 fen of silver per liang/ding. Silver would be delivered to government offices, where officials would use it to purchase the materials requisitioned by the capital; after 1548 the transportation of those materials was also undertaken by government employees rather than being arranged via corvee. 
	- If this rate was kept for a long period, its burden should have varied greatly depending on the silver:grain price ratio
- 纲银法: First developed in Fujianese counties in the Chenghua and Hongzhi years. Refers to a disparate set of regulations specifying government budgets (in terms of silver) to avoid excesses. Extended by Shen Zhuo, also in 1520, but it took until the end of the Jiajing era to iron out deficiencies and manage to solidify faithful accounting in practice, related to the merger of "miscellaneous" expenses into a single silver-denominated levy. (Li doesn't cite evidence that this really was ever successful.)
- 均徭法: Earlier versions of this reform were focused on fairly allocating labor levies, but with the introduction of silver commutation it became possible to recalculate "fair" levies of all kinds on the basis of a simple grain tax/ding to silver ratio. - Around 1537 the yushi Li Yuanyang 李元阳 implemented the 十段法, which involved calculating the proper land/labor tax for an entire county, dividing it by 10, and charging that year's taxpaying *jia* for their share in silver, with which the government would hire laborers and buy materials; this was a way to "equalize" taxes while giving local government greater oversight and control over the collection and distribution of taxes. 
- 一条鞭法: Believes that the Single Whip was implemented "smoothly" because the ground had already been prepared by the above-mentioned reforms. Sees the Single Whip as part of the tendency towards greater government oversight over the fiscal system: "综观明中期福建地区的役法改革，无论是上供物料的办纳改由官负责， 纲银的收支由官府总体调度， 亦是“一条鞭法” 后的“供其入者民， 司其出者官”."
	- Quotes the Quanzhou Fuzhi on the Single Whip: 通府州县十岁中， 夏税秋粮存留起运额若干， 纲徭兵站加银额若干， 通为一条……即库子、驿站之徭差，旧时最为民所苦者， 一概通融均派。供其入者民， 司其出者官， 而奸徒之抑勒需索无所籍手， 著为令， 甲民甚以为便。盖赋役之法， 始也烦， 继也简， 始则赋役分为二， 今则合而为一矣。
Conclusion: The formalization of expenses was not the cause of local fiscal weakness in the late Ming; rather it gave more importance and room for maneuver to local officials, who were thus able to meet the extraordinary military expenses of the era without raising taxes. 



Evolution of Mid-Ming Fujian's Tributary System
Abstract: 明中期福建地区的上供物料制度因存在物料数目剧增、所征物料非时非地、摊派负担不均的弊端，而陷入运行困境。正德十五年（15２０年）始，福建地方对上供物料制度进行了“八分法”改革，逐渐将物料与田赋合并征收，至万历初年“一条鞭法”推行，贡与赋的合并基本完成。上供物料制度改革顺应了当时商品经济发展的大势，暂时减轻了人民负担，刺激了经济发展。但物料折银并不彻底，地方有司向中央解送时仍遵循着“实物主义”的原则，即使部分物料折银，在向田赋合并时，却借机将临时加派固定化，进一步加重民众负担。

- "目前学术界对上供物料制度的整体性研究侧重于上供物料与里甲正役负担的关系, 物料折银与市场的互动, 物料增长的趋势等，在对上供物料制度进行区域性深入研究时虽揭示了上供物料制度在各地运行时的“地方性色彩”，但对其在明中期业已出现的“困境”重视不够，亦未深入探究明中叶以来全国范围内上供物料制度改革的基本内容." 39
	- Cites 赵中南．明代物料征收研究, Iwami Hiroshi, and 赵毅，丁亮．明代上供物料的增长趋势与办纳方式的变迁———以浙江为中心
- Uses the Dehua County gazetteer from the Hongzhi era to compare the trend in in-kind levies from Hongwu to Hongzhi (from 5 to 48 items). Finds that the goods demanded of Dehua in the latter period were both far more numerous and more diverse than previously. Categories included bows/arrows, paper, camphor, white and black sugar, tea, copper, iron, and multiple types of cloth. Meanwhile, the Wanli-era Fuzhou gazetteer notes that levies rose from 4 items under Hongwu, to 13 under Chenghua, to 23 in Hongzhi. 40
	- Points out that besides Zhangzhou, nowhere in Fujian was suitable for silkworms, so most parts of the province had to import raw silk from elsewhere to fill their levies of silk cloth. 
- Collapse of the lijia: 
	1. Inequalities introduced by loss of equilibrium in the populations of the various li and jia; since levies were not adjusted for the actual state of a year's jianjia, they could become an unbearable burden.
	2. In Fujian in particular, the government lost the ability to effectively take censuses, and a large part of the population thus escaped registration. Liang Fangzhong found that Fujian had lost 62% of its kou and 53.77% of its hu by the Hongzhi years, the largest drop of any region (and compared to a 100%+ increase in northern provinces).
	3. There was not enough agricultural land in Fujian for tax payment in the form of cultivated products to be feasible. In the Hongwu era land surveys Fujian had the smallest amount of cultivable land per household (17 mu). 
- After the crisis of the in-kind levy system, a series of reforms were put in place that tended towards replacing in-kind payments with monetary payments, culminating in the Single Whip. 
	- From Bafenfa to Yitiao Bianfa: 
		- The bafenfa, implemented in 1520 by Shen Zhuo, charged a flat annual fee of 8 silver fen per *shi* of grain tax and per ding; the silver would then be used to buy the needed materials. The precise value varied from place to place; 8 fen per liang/ding was just Shen's normative proposal. In 1528 the yushi Nie Bao reduced the levy to 6 fen. 
		- Shen Zhuo was not the first to use "料银" in Fujian: according to the Bamin Tongzhi (1489) it was already common practice in some parts of the province to levy silver to pay for military equipment that the weisuo no longer produced themselves (军器料银)。 
	- Yitiaobianfa
		- Quote from the Kangxi Zhangzhou Gazetteer: "：“其法通府州县十岁中夏税、秋粮存留起运额若干、均徭、里甲、土贡雇募加银额若干，通为一条总征而均支之。共征收不轮甲，通一县丁粮均派之." 
		- Notes that the yitiao bianfa not only condensed payments, it also did away with the "present jia" system by levying taxes on an annual rather than decennial basis. More regularity rather than an extraordinary burden on a given household every 10 years. 
- Two schools of thoughts on the lijia's participation in material tribute: Tang Wenji believes that submitting in-kind goods was a core part of the lijia's functions from the beginning, while Zhao Yi argues that such tribute was normally levied ad hoc on *zahu* (which might specialize in fishing, tea, etc.) before the increase in demand shifted the burden over to the lijia by the mid-Ming. 
	- 赵毅，明代上供物料的增长趋势与办纳方式的变迁———以浙江为中心
- Sees the bafen fa as an important step towards the numerization and unification of tax rates: "相较于以往的户等制而言，独立出来的丁、粮具有可量化的能力，使官吏对一系列上供物料的数字审计成为可能，同时数字化又使两者之间可折算，这就进一步为“丁”向“粮”的归并提供了基本前提." This represents a "major step" in the intellectual preparation for the Single Whip. 44
- During the Wanli years Quanzhou, Zhangzhou, and Fuzhou prefectures all started levying the taxes for their material tributes (military equipment etc) to the center in silver and buying the necessary goods on the market; they also began charging "水脚银," a customs toll which was used to cover transportation costs. 
- In the mid-Ming transportation of tribute to the capital was one of the most onerous service burdens, especially for the heaviest goods (e.g. the winter jackets that were a normal part of Fujian's tribute). 
- "万历二十年（１５９２年），福建巡抚许孚远在《申饬条鞭行各道》中更是再次重申，除了“一条鞭”之外，“不许分外一毫偏累里甲”，如有“仍前科扰及索取民间一廪一蔬，或用里甲一夫一马者，即为国法所不容”。"
	- Citing 许孚远．敬和堂集
- Believes the tributary reforms "stimulated 刺激" the commercial economy： “经过八分法的改革之后，政府手里拿到的只是白银，不得不顺应市场运作规律，将上供物料的自给性打破，获取的方式逐渐地从以赋役到以市场中来." However, this was entirely unintentional on the part of most reformers, who remained concerned with provisioning the material goods demanded by the central government of each province. 
	- ”究其根源，上供物料的折银始终是政府为维持国家机器的持续运转而引入的工具之一，其最终的目的仍是为了满足其强制的政治指令性短期需求［３７］，而无意于对社会、经济结构的长远性改造，因而其内部始终存在着复杂的新旧交织而阻滞难行。“
	- That said, combining material tribute into the land tax opened the door to constant increases in additional fees（加征）. The Fujianese writer Li Shixiong 李世熊 complained that in the late Ming that additional taxes had made things more burdensome than before the reforms (“讵料明季加征之目，几等于四差乎？”)
		- 李世熊．宁化县志 （康熙）
- A major problem of the silverizing reforms: after shifting to money instead of material goods, officials were tempted to shift treasury funds from one place to another (or simply steal them); it was often the reformers themselves who gave in to such temptations (example of Nie Bao, who use the "bafen" silver for local government expenses instead of allocating it solely to the capital tribute). 47
	- "As a result, although the silverization of tribute was able to temporarily increase collection efficiency and increase local governments' financial flexibility, a lack of effective oversight hampered the process of regularizing the management of "goods silver" and provided an opening for greedy and corrupt officials." "因而，上供物料折银虽可一时提升办纳效率、增强地方财政管理弹性，但因缺乏有效管理而阻滞了料银管理的规范化进程，使贪官污吏有机可乘."
	- Compare this to Zelin’s discussion of silver being pulled between central and local governments
