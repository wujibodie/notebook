## Bai Dong
白栋 - 陕西米脂人. Jinshi LQ 5. 道御史、东阿知县. Known for implementing the Single Whip method.
## Cao Daye
曹大野 - 四川巴县人. Jinshi LQ 2. 江西巡抚、给事中. Known for impeaching Gao Gong, allegedly instigated by Zhang Juzheng.

## Cao Xuequan 
曹学佺 (能始）- 福建福州候館縣人. Jinshi WL 23. Book collector and Donglin partisan who had an undistinguished official career but in old age held a high position in the court of the Longwu emperor, before committing suicide when Fuzhou fell to the Qing. Wrote a yeshi on the Wanli reign (野史纪略), and voluminous commentaries and selections on poetry. Considered himself a follower of Li Zhi and Jiao Hong, and corresponded with Xie Zhaozhe. 
- “……语云:‘始乎简，终毕乎巨。君子以察浸祥，以审风俗，召好去恶，于斯乎在，乌可苟哉？’”(《石仓全集·夜光堂近稿》
## Chen Chen
陳琛 (思獻，紫峯) - 福建泉州晉江人. Jinshi JD 12. Student of Cai Qing's. Commentator on the classics and associate of Zhang Yue and Lin Xiyuan. Retired early and spent most of his energy on teaching, writing, and local projects in Jinjiang. Both Lin and Zhang left muzhiming for him. 
- Daoguang Jinjiang gazetteer bio: 陈琛，字思献，号紫峰，蔡虚斋高弟也。初学于李聪，聪引琛学于虚斋。虚斋异之，尝语琛曰：“吾所发愤涵泳而仅得者，不意子皆已得之。”屈引辈与为礼，琛固辞，乃师事焉。及督学江右，请琛偕行，教其二子。归而设科学宫之旁，与郡城月台寺，四方从学甚众。所著有《四书浅说》、《易经通典》一书。正德庚午，领乡荐。丁丑成进士。考官编修尹褒持琛卷，荐主考靳大学士贵，靳反复数遍曰：“必出陈白沙门下，或师虚斋，他人不能为此。”释褐，授刑部山西司主事。以母老，乞改南，得户部差监淮安舟税。寻转吏部考功郎，得封赠。琛曰：“持此足以慰母矣。”遂乞归养。嘉靖七年，以大臣荐，诏征用，辞。八年，即家拜贵州按察提学佥事，复改江西，皆辞不赴。**乡有利病当兴革者，如六里陂水利，八里亭迤南至龟湖道路，言于有司，浚筑缮治，乡人德之。** 归养十二年，母终。琛年几六十，后十一年卒。有司特祠之学宫。
## Chen Hongmo
陳洪謨 - 湖廣武陵人. Jinshi ZD 9. 漳州知府 during the Zhengde era. Mentioned in Zhangzhou gazetteers in connection with educational and religious institutions and the development of the prefecture. Represents early 16th century Zhangzhou administration before the wokou crisis.
## Chen Jianwu
陳見吾 - 湖光江陵人. Xunfu of Yunnan. Zhang wrote to discuss infrastructure works in Yunnan.
## Chen Rui
陈瑞 - 福建长乐人. Jinshi JJ 32. 两广总督，湖廣巡撫（萬曆３－６), 兵部尚书. Involved in relief efforts for flood victims in Gong'an county and suppression of bandits in Guangdong. Eventually befriended and supportsed Matteo Ricci. Impeached in 1583 as part of anti-Zhang purges.
## Chen Sheng
陈省 - 福建长乐人. Jinshi JJ 38. 湖广巡抚. Known for heavy bribes to Zhang Juzheng and providing military protection for Zhang's family.
## Fang Fengshi
方逢時 （行之，金湖）- 湖廣嘉魚人. Jinshi JJ 20. Bingbu shangshu, Xuanda Zongdu, Datong Xunfu. Picked by Zhang to replace Wang Chonggu as Xuanda Zongdu. Appreciated by Zhang, along with Wang, as key official in the northern border defense strategy.
## Fu Zuozhou
傅作舟 - 湖广江陵县人. Jinshi LQ 5. 光禄寺少卿、南京给事中、知县. Supported currency reform and involved in disaster relief and evaluations.
## Gao Cai
高寀 - 宦官 (eunuch). Active in Fujian maritime customs in the late Wanli period. Blamed for provoking the Manila massacre of 1603 through his taxation policies on grain merchants' boats. His interference in maritime affairs exemplifies the complications that followed Liu Yaohui's more measured approach to maritime customs. Connected to Yu Zigao's misadventures in Manila and Penghu.
## Gao Guangxian
郜光先 - 山西长治人. Jinshi JJ 38. 陕西三边总督、右佥都御史巡抚延绥. Managed tribute markets, border inspections, and Mongol affairs.
## Gao Wenjian
高文荐 - 陕西凤翔人. Jinshi JJ 38. 右佥都御史巡抚山西、山西右布政使. Focused on border defense against Anda and construction of border fortresses.
## Geng Dingxiang
耿定向 - 湖北黃安人. Jinshi JJ 35. Supporter of Zhang Juzheng. Hosted Li Zhi before falling out with him. Xunfu of Fujian from Wanli 6 to Wanli 8. Huang Zongxi placed him in the Taizhou School (bio on 355). Zhang Juzheng referred to him as a friend among the Hanlin scholars (答列鄉毛介川).
- [[Ming Shi Biographies#Geng Dingxiang]]
## Guo Siji
郭思极 - 直隶魏县人. Jinshi LQ 2. 巡抚应天右佥都御史，湖广巡按御史. Notorious for admitting Zhang Juzheng's son Zhang Maoxiu in provincial examinations and executing He Xinyin.
## Guo Yingpin
郭应聘 - 福建莆田人. Jinshi JJ 29. 兵部尚书、右都御史兼兵部右侍郎总督两广军务. Specialist in Southwestern military affairs. Started in Guangxi under Yin Zhengmao 殷正茂, then served as Guangxi Xunfu under Liu Yaohui during latter's time as Liangguang Zongdu; later served as Zongdu himself. Suppressed rebellions in Guangxi including Gutian bandits and Yao people in Fujiang and Huaiyuan. Per Mingshi bio, was an admirer of Wang Yangming and associate of Hai Rui.
## Hai Rui
海瑞 - 廣東瓊山人. Jinshi JJ 29.
Acquired fame for his bold remonstrance to the Jiajing emperor, for which he was briefly jailed and then rehabilitated in the Longqing reign.
## He Kuan
何寬 - 浙江台州人. Jinshi JJ 29. 福建巡撫(1569-71).
## He Qiaoyuan
何喬遠 (稚孝，匪蓮) - 福建泉州晉江人. Jinshi WL 14. 禮部右侍郎. Quanzhou literatus whose writings (鏡山全集, 閩書, 海上小議, 開洋海議) provide crucial accounts of Minnan maritime trade, the wokou crisis, and relations with overseas Chinese communities. An ardent supporter of Zheng Zhilong in the Quanzhou gentry. His correspondence with Zheng Zhilong and advocacy for maritime trade opening make him essential for understanding late Ming Fujian's commercial-official networks.
## He Qiming
何起鸣 - 四川内江人. Jinshi JJ 38. 工部尚书、工部侍郎、大理寺卿、右副都御史巡抚山东. Handled the An Guoxing rebellion in Guizhou and land surveys in Shandong.
## Hong Chaoxuan
洪朝選 - 福建同安人. Jinshi JJ 25. Tong'an local who served as 右副都御史 in the 1560s and 70s. According to wiki he was involved in the legal dispute between [劉夢龍] and [何寬]; he was eventually arrested and committed suicide in prison in Wanli 9, which eventually became the pretext for the extirpation of [Zhang Juzheng]'s family. According to a story in the Ming Shi (孫瑋) the specific crime Hong was accused of was consorting with foreigners (通夷 — not 倭！).
## Hong Fangzhou
洪芳洲 (朝选) - 福建 泉州 同安人. 刑部侍郎. Served as 右副都御史 in 1560s/70s. Per the QZFZ, erected an 御史中丞坊 in Tong'an. Imprisoned in the lawsuit between Liu Menglong and Hong's relatives in Tong'an, which imbricated [[#Pang Shanpeng]].
## Hu Shouren
胡守仁 (子安，近塘) - 浙江觀海衛人。總兵 of Fujian, praised by Liu Yaohui and impeached by Pang Shangpeng. The commander tasked by Liu with chasing down Lin Feng.
## Hu Zhili
胡执礼 - 陕西永昌人. Jinshi JJ 38. 右副都御史巡抚应天、太常寺卿. Reformed tax and corvee systems in Wu region and provided disaster relief.
## Hu Zongxian
胡宗憲 (梅林) - 安徽績溪人. Jinshi JJ 17. 兵部右侍郎，總督浙直福建軍務. Supreme Commander of the coordinated anti-wokou campaigns in Zhejiang during the 1550s-60s, overseeing Qi Jiguang and Yu Dayou's professional armies. Established the model of silverized mercenary forces (客兵) that would become central to Fujian's military structure. Protected by Yan Song until the latter's fall; thereafter impeached by Liu Yaohui among others, stripped of rank, and eventually died in prison. His tenure represents the critical transition from derelict weisuo garrisons to professionalized coastal defense.
## Huang Fengxiang
黃鳳翔 - 福建泉州晉江人. Jinshi LQ 2. 南京禮部尚書. Most successful Minnan official of his day. Wrote a stele inscription on the Anhai 駐鎮. Opposed Zhang Juzheng's repressions of critical officials in the duoqing controversy. Edited an edition of the Quanzhou Fuzhi.
- Wrote a 嘉靖政宜.
- His preface to the Quanzhou Gazetteer begins on p. 23 of my pdf version.
## Huo Yuxia
霍與霞 (子璧，勉齋) - 廣東南海縣人. Jinshi JJ 38. Son of Huo Tao. Appears in the letters of Yu Dayou, Pang Shangpeng, and associated with Hai Rui as young officials in Zhejiang. In and out of office in the Longqing/Wanli years, and ultimately spent most of his prime retired at home. Wrote on the Portuguese in Macao.
## Jia Yingyuan
贾应元 - 直隶顺天遵化人. Jinshi JJ 41. 兵部侍郎、宣大巡抚、右佥都御史巡抚大同. Handled relations with Anda, Tuman, and Huang Taiji, and constructed border walls.
## Jiao Hong
焦竑 (弱候，澹園）- 南京旗手衛人. Zhuangyuan in WL 17. Mostly held Hanlin and examiner appointments, with a brief banishment as 福寧州同知. Student of Geng Dingxiang and associate of Li Zhi's. Author of a number of historical works, including the biographical encyclopedia 國朝獻徵錄.
## Lao Kan
劳堪 - 江西德华人. Jinshi JJ 35. Xunfu of Fujian Wanli 8-9. Arrested Hong Chaoxuan and was later censured and punished for it.
## Li Guangjin
李光縉 - 福建泉州晉江人. Quanzhou literatus, author of 景璧集. Provides important accounts of Anping merchants, including the biography of Li Yuxi (who escaped the 1603 Manila massacre because he anticipated the eunuch-driven disaster) and the porcelain merchant 狀天夔. His writings are key sources for understanding Minnan merchant life and overseas trade networks.
## Li Shida
李世达（渐庵）- 陕西泾阳人. Jinshi JJ 35. Xunfu of Shandong, Zhejiang, ended up as Zuodu Yushi (thus the anachronistic title of 总宪 in Zhang's letters.)
## Li Youzi
李幼滋 - 湖广应城人. Jinshi JJ 26. 工部尚书、湖南按察使、关中宪使、工科给事中. Supported Pan Jixun's river management and was involved in the duoqing controversy.
## Li Zhi
李贄 (卓吾，宏甫) - 福建泉州晉江人. Jinshi JJ 31. 姚安知府. The most famous heterodox thinker from Minnan, known for his 焚書 and engagement with commerce, Buddhism, and moral philosophy. Returned to Quanzhou in 1560 after his father's death and describes defending city walls from wokou. Hosted by Geng Dingxiang at Huang'an before their famous public falling-out. His writings provide important first-person accounts of wokou-era Quanzhou and represent a distinctive intellectual tradition counter to the Cheng-Zhu orthodoxy of Lin Xiyuan. Eventually arrested on charges of heterodoxy and died in prison (Wanli 30).
## Liang Menglong
梁夢龍 (鳴泉) - 河北真定人. Jinshi JJ 32. Xunfu of Henan, Shandong; Bingbu Shangshu. Zhang introduced to Xiong Fu via letter.
## Lin Feng
林鳳 - 廣東饒平人. The principal pirate antagonist in Liu Yaohui's tenure as Fujian Xunfu. Rose to prominence during the 1560s wokou conflicts; by the early 1570s commanded a substantial fleet operating across Fujian-Guangdong waters. In late 1574, led an expedition of 2,000-4,000 men across 60+ vessels to attack the nascent Spanish settlement at Manila. Repelled by Spanish forces, he retreated to Pangasinan and was besieged; escaped autumn 1575 with ~1,000 followers and returned to Chaozhou to rebuild through coastal raids. His pursuit triggered the 剿 vs. 招撫 conflict between Fujian and Guangdong authorities that consumed the final year of Liu Yaohui's tenure. Ultimate fate unclear—reportedly fled "西番" after 1576.
- Primary sources: Liu Yaohui's memorials (馀賦未殄乞行專剿疏); Ming Shilu; Mingshi biography of Ling Yunyi; Spanish sources (Chirino, Colin, Morga)
## Lin Xiyuan
林希元 （次崖）- 泉州同安人. Jinshi ZD 12. Most famous in his lifetime for his Yijing textbook 易經存疑. Involved closely in overseas commerce after retiring home and finding himself in genteel poverty. Among the Fujian gentry accused by Zhu Wan of cooperating with smugglers. Close associate and patron of Yu Dayou.
- 同安林次崖先生文集， Toyo Bunko V-5-C-235
- Four volumes of his writings are in the Ming Jingshi Wenbian.
## Lin Yingshun
林应训 - 福建侯官人. Jinshi LQ 5. 南京尚宝司卿、直隶巡按御史. Reopened Lianhu Lake, built gates at Benniu, and cleared occupied beaches for water conservancy projects.
## Ling Yunyi
凌雲翼 (洋山) - 南直隸太倉州人. Jinshi JJ 26. 兵部尚書，兩廣總督. Zongdu of Liangguang during the latter phase of the Lin Feng campaigns (succeeding Yin Zhengmao). His Mingshi biography provides key details about Hu Shouren's pursuit of Lin Feng and Wang Wanggao's embassy to Luzon. Successfully completed the pacification of Luopang. Known for aggressive military tactics but also criticized for excessive violence.
- [[Ming Shi Biographies#Ling Yunyi]]
## Liu Daichuan
劉帶川 （燾）- Zongdu of Liangguang; appointed in Longqing 3 to assist Xiong Fu.

## Liu Ji 
劉畿 （子京，羽泉) - 直隸蘇州府長洲縣人. Jinshi JJ 29. Zhejiang Xunfu, 總督浙直江西
. Operated in Zhejiang from JJ 43 - 5, d. LQ 1; coordinated closely with Pan Jixun and Pang Shangpeng (Xun'an Yushi at the same time). Involved in fiscal retrenchment after the peak of wokou crisis had passed, in cooperation with Single Whip reformers, but also helped coordinate massive trans-provincial campaigns against Wu Ping. Represents the frugal consolidation phase (节俭) after Hu Zongxian's work, though generally followed Hu's policies (e.g. trade prohibitions). His 督撫疏議十卷 is a detailed source on coastal defense and fiscal policy in the Zhejiang of the very late Jiajing years (~1564-6).
- As demobilization measure, converted mercenary forces to agricultural militia (義烏官兵回縣且耕且練), creating the "Yiwu model" of military-agricultural integration.

## Liu Xuan
刘铉 - 江西鄱阳人. Jinshi LQ 2. 江陵知县. Known for reforming lijia system, equalizing taxes, and promoting education.
## Liu Yaohui
刘尧诲（晦)（凝斋) - 湖广临武人. Jinshi JJ 32. Fujian Xunfu before Pang Shangpeng. The first Xunfu of Fujian appointed by ZJZ in Wanli 1-4. Later moved to Liangguang Zongdu, continued to be in frequent contact with Zhang, particularly regarding battles against Lin Feng, in the management of whom Liu seems to have been Zhang's trusted expert. Shared a philosophy with Zhang of rooting out corruption and advancing talents in the military.
- His personal writings are the [虚籁集](https://www.shidianguji.com/book/SK0000A/chapter/1kgttf14g7tbm)
- The 明史 (Hu Zongxian's bio) mentions that he impeached Hu Zongxian, who at the time was still "protected" by Yan Song, causing Liu's dismissal for 10 years; however Huang Fengxiang's version of events doesn't mention Liu, so he presumably wasn't too important among those attacking Hu at the time.
## Liu Yingjie
劉應節 （北川) - 山東濰人. Zongdu of Ji-Liao.
## Luo Yueyan
羅月巖 - A friend of Zhang's; Zhang wrote to console him after he was demoted.
## Lv Tiaoyang
呂調陽 - 廣西桂林人. Jinshi JJ 17. 禮部，吏部尚書. Zhang Juzheng wrote an epitaph for him, as well as letters in his wenji.
## Ma Ziqiang
马自强 - 陕西同州人. Jinshi JJ 32. 太子太保兼文渊阁大学士、礼部尚书. Revised the Zongfan Regulations and strictly implemented tribute and market rewards with Anda.
## Mao Kai
毛愷 (介川) - 浙江 衢州 江山人. Shangshu for the Xing, Libu. A frequent correspondent of Zhang's. Zhang once criticized Geng Dingxiang to Mao, per Geng's hurt letter to Zhang. Also a correspondent of Yu Dayou's.

## Mao Kun 
茅坤 (順甫, 鹿門) - 浙江 湖州 歸安人. JJ 17 Jinshi. 廣西兵備僉事. Gained a reputation for military expertise during time in Guangxi, but was censured for corruption. Then became a follower of Hu Zongxian (胡宗憲延之幕中), who requested Mao's appointment to Fujian as 副使, but Pang Shangpeng (then investigating Zhejiang) accused Mao's relatives of corruption and he was cashiered. In retirement he went into business and became wealthy. Father of Mao Wei, the publisher. 

## Pan Jixun
潘季驯 （印川） - 浙江 湖州 乌程人. JJ 29 jinshi. Pushed the 均平法 in Guangdong in the early 1560s. From 1565-1592 repeatedly served as 河道总领, and was respected as the foremost hydraulic expert of his age. 
## Pan Sheng
潘晟 (思明，木簾) - 浙江新昌人. Jinshi JJ 20. 禮部尚書. Urged Zhang to duoqing.
## Pang Shangpeng
龐尚鵬 (少南，惺菴) - 廣東南海人. JJ 32 jinshi. Xunfu of Fujian from Wanli 4 to Wanli 6. First letter from Zhang was received while still charged with the salt monopoly; Zhang approved of a memorial 鹽政二十事，屯政便宜數十事. Pang was removed from salt post despite Zhang's support. Later appointed Xunfu of Fujian with mandate to implement the Single Whip, but was removed after sending Zhang a letter urging him to go into mourning.

## Zhu Liangbao
朱良寶 - Pirate fleet commander identified alongside Lin Feng as an immediate threat in Liu Yaohui's first comprehensive memorial (autumn 1573). Operating across the Guangdong border. Represents the broader pirate networks that Fujian authorities had to contend with beyond the single figure of Lin Feng.
## Zhu Wan
朱紈 (子純) - 浙江長興人. Jinshi ZD 9. 浙江巡撫，提督浙閩海防軍務. The zealous anti-piracy official of the Jiajing reign who prosecuted Fujian gentry accused of cooperating with smugglers, including members of Lin Xiyuan's circle. His aggressive enforcement of the maritime ban created powerful enemies among coastal elites; he was eventually impeached, stripped of office, and committed suicide (JJ 29). His fate symbolizes the difficulty of enforcing prohibition policies against entrenched local interests and serves as essential background for understanding the accommodation policies that followed.
## Zhu Ying
朱英 (時傑) - 湖廣桂陽人. Jinshi Zhengtong 10. In the 1460s, responsible for spreading the junyao reform to Guangdong, Fujian, and Shaanxi as 布政使.
## Zhu Zhengse
朱正色 - 直隶南和人. Jinshi WL 2. 右佥都御史巡抚宁夏赞理军务、陕西副使、江陵知县. Per Feng Ming, "implemented the Single Whip method."
