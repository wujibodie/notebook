- Basic question of the whole book, in Iwai's words: "What sort of mutual relationship existed between the long-term fiscal structure and socio-economic activities?" Very similar language and questions to Liu Zhiwei; who influenced who? 13 
- Overall core concept of the whole book is the interweaving of "fixed quotas" and "floating assessments" in Ming-Qing tax policy; devotion to the ideal of fixed quotas in theory made reliance on floating, flexible levies inevitable in practice. 
Conclusion to Chapter 5 - Zhu Yuanzhang and his advisors likely never intended the *yaoyi* to fill a fiscal function, and so did not have the possibility of increases to the levies in mind when designing the lijia system. As a result, when revenue collection imperatives were actually tacked on to the lijia, some amount of friction was inevitable. Since additional pressures were being added in the form of extraction in the "blind spots" of the law, it was also natural that the ordinary practice of the lijia would adjust in various unexpected ways. Adding on the constant pressure to somehow remain faithful to the "founding will" of the dynasty, the fiscal system became enormously convoluted and opaque; but the fundamental problem remained the internally contradictory systems within it. These contradictions should be the starting point for a study of the evolution of the labor service system in the Ming. 
- "明太祖朱元璋及其顾问们创立的国家货币，财政体系中也许并不一定把徭役的征收作为核心目标来构思里甲制。如果这样的话，那么源于货币，财政体系结构而必然要发生的追加性，附加性的税役的加重，与没有预见到税役加重的情况而设定的里甲制的原则，功能等之间产生摩擦，也是理所当然的了。而且，在法律制度的盲区不断加重的徭役科派的压力下，里甲制以及地方行政制度一方也会在法律制度的构架中巧妙地改变实质内容来顺应压力。这种压力的大小与顺应的方法不一而足，除此之外还存在着一种维持开国之初的“祖法"所定制度的压力，这些因素都使明代税，役制度变得复杂与不透明，而最根本的还是货币，财政体系内部存在着制度性的紧张关系。如果这样的话，那么对这种紧张关系的理解首先应当成为我们历史地解读明代徭役问题发展变化的立足点。"
