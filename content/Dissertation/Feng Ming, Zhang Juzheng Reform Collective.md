Chapter 2: 張居正改革群體構成
- Classic perspective on Zhang Juzheng, given by himself and his son Jingxiu as well as Wei Qingyuan, is that he did not form 黨 and largely relied on his own talents and power to push through his reforms.
- 劉台, on the other hand, accused Zhang of favoring Chu natives and private friends, while others wrote of 居正之黨. Feng sees the Zhang party and the reform collective as overlapping but distinct. 43
- Early events in the formation of Zhang‘s groups: 
	- Zhang supports 殷正茂, who is made Xunfu of Guangxi in 69 and defeats a Zhuang rebellion.
	- Zhang supports 兩廣總督張瀚 despite his failure to put down Zeng Yiben; Yin Maocheng then puts down the bandits in 1571. 
	- Zhang approves Wang Chonggu‘s plan to use Altan Khan‘s grandson as a bargaining chip to 封貢 Altan and bring peace to the north frontier (1570). 
	- Supports Tan Lun and Qi Jiguang in their promotions and pushes for their transfer to the north. 
	- In the „coup“ of Wanli 1, Zhang cements his alliance with Empress Li and Feng Bao, as well as with a number of officials he elevates at this times, especially 王國光，譚綸，王之誥. 
		- Ge Shouli is appointed 左右都御史 of the Gongbu at this time, but Feng leaves him out of the reform group.
- The 2 subordinate Neige members at Zhang‘s time, 呂調陽 + 張四維 were both mild personalities and supporters of Zhang. 
-  Officials who fell out w/ Zhang over the 奪情 affair flocked to support either Zhang Siwei or Shen Sixing. Feng Bao was eventually forced out, further eliminating some of Zhang‘s old supporters. 
- Most important period of 清算 was in years 82-84, when Zhang himself came under posthumous attack. In all 28 people were removed from high office in the years 82-87. Only 6 members of the „reform collective“ remained in office and loyal to Zhang‘s memory after 1587.
