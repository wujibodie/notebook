
Xu by 葉向高 of Fuqing. Contents start with a series of prefaces, then ji, then a 聖訓敷言八則 (exhortations to study; first one states that unrelenting study, not natural intelligence, is what separates the wise from foolish, etc. ), a small amount of philosophizing correspondence. Little that seems to refer directly to his time in charge of Fujian.
