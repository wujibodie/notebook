與閩中巡撫劉凝齋
- The first letter to Liu on his assignment to Fujian. "閩中數年無警，當事者務潔節省虛名，以致緩急無措，誤事非小。" Calls 俞大猷 "老奸", and suggests using Hu Shouren instead. 
- 闽中数年无警，当事者务沽节省虚名，以致缓急无措，误事非小。公所请者，已属该部一一覆行。俞帅老奸，志意隳，难以复用，非新壮将军，不能办此。胡君旧在闽中，颇著战功，锐于功名，惟公结以诚信，激以忠义，必能有所建也。用兵机宜，难以遥度，瓯岭以东，一付于公。
答閩中巡撫劉凝齋
- A note on Lin Feng; in other letters of this section discusses the situation with Yin in Guangdong. 
- 林贼既已入彀，果不出闽人得之之料，喜甚，喜甚。但当其时，即宜少出闽师以助之。夷情多变，死贼围久，或生计，谅此时成败已决矣，倾耳以俟捷音。
答閩中巡撫劉凝齋
- 屡辱翰示，及别楮所云，一一领悉。顷闻有贵恙，无任怀念，想勿药矣。近据闽、广所报贼形皆溃乱奔窜之状，凤似不在其中，毙于吕宋之说，恐不虚也。果尔，皆闽人之功。若非遣谍行间，彼番人安肯歼乎？今想已有的耗，倾耳以捷音之至。 
答閩中巡撫劉凝齋
- 闽师千里赴难，虽无所获，犹当论功，况斩馘黎丹，灼然耳目者乎！广人欲来招抚之议，忘大德而逞小忿，曲有所。圣明远见万里，功罪赏罚，不爽秋毫。本兵已覆勘议，幸公勿以为介。但凤贼存亡，尚无的耗，仍望公之留意也。

答兩廣劉凝齋
- Liu is  appointed Liangguang Zongdu after Lin Feng returns to that province and catches the local authorities unawares; Liu had been right about the petty and short-sighted officials of Liangguang failing to take proper precautions. Thus Liu is sent to Guangdong to clean up the mess, explicitly being asked to follow on his successes as Fujian Xunfu.
- 顷林贼复回广东，彼中无素备，其文武将吏又皆庸驽，竟令纵洋而去，殊可恨也。以公昔在闽中，当留意于此。而人又皆乐为公用，故特借重一行。且广中吏治偷窳，近稍稍振刷，而积习未殄，望公之留意也。然以公重望，乃不引置廊庙，而复令徊翔于远徼，区区又有蔽贤之咎矣。 
答兩廣劉凝齋
- 窃闻志士不违时而赴功，忠臣不择地而求效。顷者，借重粤中，实以远地为歉。兹奉华翰，欣然以王忠肃诸公自待，此忠臣智士所以乘时而自奋者也。以公宏抱，其所建立，王、马诸公岂足方驾哉？浣慰，浣慰。 辱示诸疏，尤极仰服。夫捕盗之令，布之天下，非不申熟也，而惟江右奉行之不后。江右司道奉公之令，非不申也，而惟二道举行之有效。以此见，法无常良，行之在人耳。公今治粤，亦岂有异道哉？任人责实，信赏必罚而已。
答两广刘凝斋条经略海寇四事 
- Detailed memo to Liu as Liangguang Zongdu; returns to Liu's original strategy for capturing Lin Feng, with the matter entrusted to Hu Shouren. Also approves of the proposal to garrison Nan'ao, despite Guangdong people's complaints about the distance + subordination to Min authorities (!). Fourth point: the fundamental cause for Guangdong's restiveness is official corruption and waste, and so the solution is frugality, oversight, and supporting the peoples' livelihoods. Though progress has been made, the Guang-Min border is still especially troublesome, due in part to local connivance with the bandits: 最可患者，与闽接壤，彼中洁廉者少，又党与众盛，朋比为奸，法令难行，不得不借惠文冠弹治。
- 伻来，知已荣代，入粤有期，欣慰。别具广事数件，以公雄略，固所优为，然不肖有四方之虑，不敢不丁宁于下执也。仰惟鉴存。 
	一、广中防御山盗，闻已略备，惟海防甚疏。昨林道乾以丧败群寇，倏泊河渡，使该道有人，武备稍预，缚而致之耳。乃竟无一兵一船，使之从容扬帆而去，可恨，可恨！今亡羊而补牢亦未晚也。 
	一、林贼前已入彀，以闽、广两处购之，彼此争功，遂致败谋。而闽人去者皆被荼毒，殊为可恨。顷已将此事密付呼帅。公旧在闽中，首事呼帅，又意所推毂者，必为效死。今仍宜付之密图，在广人唯当多方设备，张罗以待鸟而已。 
	一、南澳设将，本公昔所建白，乃近多言不便者。此必广人惮于远戍，又苦闽中牵制耳。然论事势，守堂奥者必于外，据险扼要，乃为得策。此地实海中要害，昨林道乾一来，径泊海岸，使此地兵将有备，渠敢越乎？公虚心审计，果防守有益，幸为之规画久远，措处兵食，使人有乐居之意，则不强之而自从。倘无大关系，则亦不嫌于前后异议也。 
	一、广中数年多盗，非民之好乱，本于吏治不清，贪官为害耳。夫官贪，则良民不怀，奸民不畏，而盗贼利足以之，威足以慑之，何惮而不为盗！今朝廷法纪稍振，贪风稍戢，盗亦渐少，然习未尽变也。最可患者，与闽接壤，彼中洁廉者少，又党与众盛，朋比为奸，法令难行，不得不借惠文冠弹治。故今日治广，武备固当振饬，然节财用，察吏治安民生，乃其要者也。幸惟留神。

答两广刘凝斋

答福建巡抚耿楚侗谈王霸之辩
- Zhang is busy printing the 纶简汇编 (?) at the same time as he is trying to fulfill the emperor's will, pacify the people, and keep the military funded. Praises Geng's letter for its deep insight on governance and the problems of the time. When Wu Wanghu criticized Zhang for only focusing on 富国强兵 rather than 行帝王之道, Zhang laughed and said: 旺湖过誉我矣！吾安能使国富兵强哉？Confucius, Shun, and Zhou Gong all said that sufficient food and strength was the basis of the state; only impractical later scholars lost sight of this. Even after 7 or 8 years in power, Zhang feels that the state's problems have hardly improved; in a crisis the state's resources would be insufficient for the granaries and soldiers; thanks Geng for continuing to trust him. One must be careful and practical in order to be effective. 第须一一核实考成，乃可有效.

答福建巡抚耿楚侗言致理安民
- 丈田赈饥驿传诸议、读之再三、心快然如有所获、盖治理之道。莫要于安民。究观前代。孰不以百姓安乐而阜康。闾阎愁苦而危乱者。当嘉靖中年。商贾在位。货财上流。百姓嗷嗷。莫必其命。比时景象。曾有异于汉唐之末世乎。幸赖　祖宗德泽深厚。民心爱戴巳久。仅免危亡耳。隆庆间仕路稍清。民始帖席。而纪纲不振。弊习尚存。虗文日繁。寔惠益寡。天启　圣明。虽在冲幼。留心治理。仆每思本朝立国矢见模。章程法度。尽善尽美。远过汉唐。至于宋之儒弱牵制。尤难并语。今不必复有纷更。惟仰法我　高皇帝怀保小民一念。用以对越　上帝。奠安国本耳。故自受事以来。凡朝夕之所入告。教令之所敷布。惓惓以是为务。锄强戮凶。剔奸厘革。有不得巳而用威者。惟欲以安民而巳。奸人不便于巳。猥言时政苛猛。以摇惑众听。江陵最不乐讲学诸公而迂阔虗谈之士。动引晚宋衰乱之政。以抑损　上德。矫捍文罔。不知我　祖宗神威圣德。元与宋不同。哺糟拾馀。无裨实用。徒以惠奸宄。贼良民耳。世儒达治者尠。虽勉遵　上令。而实未得于心。所以宣上达下者。苟以文具矢见免罪责而巳。比见公诸所条布、训辞虽若严整、而肫肫爱民之意、蔼然于言外、以是服公之高识宏抱、非世儒所能及也愿益自信而坚持之。监军道裁革为便、彭湖贼未必即是真倭、但严备以待之、不必勤于远也、人旋附复、拙稿末卷有归政本末、谨附一览. Included in Huang Ming Jingshi Bian. 
- Zhang was pleased by three proposals of Geng's on land surveying, famine relief, and the postal service. The most important task of governance is to provide peace for the people. In the Jiajing years, merchants rose in status and people left their occupations. Things have improved slightly, but bad customs remain. The original Ming laws were almost perfect, surpassing the Han and Tang, and Hongwu's basic principle was protecting the common people; there is no need to introduce any more complexity than that (critiquing the Song Confucians). Zhang affirms this principle, and says that if he has been harsh, it was only with the aim of creating peace; yet scoundrels complain about "tyranny." Those who use the Song Confucians to criticize current policies don't understand that the principles of Ming governance are entirely different from those of the (failed) late Song. Ends by approving again of Geng's proposals, which are harsh on the surface but are fully intended to benefit the people. On military matters, some bandits on Penghu, while not "real Wo," should be dealt with harshly, but there is no need to pursue them if they flee to distant waters. He also encloses an essay on governance 归政本末.

答西夏直指耿楚侗 (1284)
- (From JJ41, before Zhang’s time as Shoufu) Starts by proclaiming that in these urgent times he has no time for idle words 知虚见空谈之无益. Moves on to complain about the dire state of things in the capital, where the "chessboard" is constantly changing, corruption reigns, and the people's worries worsen each day. The only thing that can solve the situation is for a straightforward and lofty gentleman to break through the impasse and clean things up. But even if such a person existed, he would not necessarily be recognized, and even then may not be used. 
	- Zhang emphasizes here that he is sharing candid feelings to a 知己, not to be shared with others -- even at this early point in his career Zhang saw Geng as a close friend. 
- Closes by asking for information on Geng's posting in Shaanxi (Xi Xia). Can the border hold? In the past this region produced heroes -- what about now? 
答羅近溪宛陵尹 (1288)
- Philosophical discussion with Luo Jinxi in which Zhang says he envies his friends who have been sent to posts outside the capital, which Zhang has never done; explains that, as a believer that “學問既知頭腦，須窺實際, ” he rejects the possibility of intuitive knowledge without hands on experience, and so encourages Luo to pay attention to the things around him rather than relying on 本覺.
	- Repeatedly uses Buddhist vocabulary, but seemingly in contrast to his own epistemology. 
- Zhang starts by saying he has three "zhiji" who are 星散 around the empire: Geng Dingxiang in Nan Zhili, Hu Lushan in Sichuan, and Luo in Ningguo.
