
松本善海 - 中国地方自治的发展史 （1977)
- Yuan as a turning point in institutional history. Sees many of Zhu Yuanzhang's centralizing reforms as drawing from the Mongols (as a minority ruling Han majority), as did the long-term division of local authority. 
	- Although Hongwu abolished the 行中书省, he kept the 省 more or less intact as highest-level admin division, as the scope of authority of the 布政使司. The 府 became the “各州县的结合体". 
- Hongwu 9 (1376) set the first rates for commutation to silver, silk, and chao. 
- The huang ce only listed households liable to regular land tax (special registrations being kept on separate records). Orphans, widows etc. were attached to li as ”fractional househoulds" 畸零户, as were sojourners residing outside their home li. 
- 均瑶法: First implemented in Zhengtong 1 by 江西佥事夏时, expanded to most of country by Tianshun years, formally extended everywhere in Hongzhi 1. Added a 均瑶册 to the 黄册 separating households into 3 or 9 ranks and specifying the corvee tasks they were to perform at the 5 year mark between their lijia corvee. However, failed to eliminate arbitrarily imposed 杂乏役, instead creating a new middle category between those and standard lijia corvee.
- The condition for the lijia's reliance on households as main fiscal unit was preponderance of independent owner-farmers. This was a long-term fact of Chinese history, but it was intensified by the land divisions of the Yuan-Ming transition. Thus the lijia started to break down as land was re-concentrated in the hands of non-taxpayers; which process was in turn linked to market silverization, which both enabled and necessitated fiscal monetarization. 
- Emphasizes the Zhengtong 1 silver commutation of tax grain from the south. Notes that the 4 dun - 1 liang ratio was kept regardless of fluctuations in market rates. As commutation became standard the accepted logic was that "贫者出力，富者出财," each according to their surplus. 
- Over time the 黄册，a primarily household registration document, lost contact with realities of land ownership, largely due to lack of real interest in proper resurveying. 
- The main thrust of the Jiajing fiscal debates was simplification of an overly complex system, and this goal led naturally to the simplification of levies and to silverization. 
	- Cites 清水泰次 that the core aim of the Single Whip was to combine 税 and 役. 
	- Two kinds of anti-Single Whip complaints: 1. moving all taxation to the land was hardest for small farmer-proprietors; 2. it forced small farmers onto the market for tax silver. Both conditions affected the north more than the south. The common perception that Zhang Juzheng extended the Single Whip to the whole country by 1581 is mistaken -- in fact he only began the land resurvey which would have laid the foundation for the Single Whip, but died before this moved past an 意向. 22
- The new village communities established by Hongwu required 里老 elders as intermediaries. But when the lijia groupings split "natural villages" the authority of such elders was undermined -- repeated by the ten-year redrawing of li boundaries.
	- With property concentration, new strata of authority emerged within the 村落自治体 which could dominate the elders.
- The village elders lost their moral-exhortative duties to the xiangyue and baojia, alongside losing their fiscal duties with the Single Whip. 
	- 保甲 had been suggested by Hongwu, but were first implemented in Fujian in the Zhengtong years by 御史 柳华 due to mine banditry. The first larger-scale implementation was by Wang Shouren in Jiangxi. 
	- In 1591 吕坤 implemented a combined xiangyue and baojia, in which groups of 100 families appointed both a 约正 and a 保正, the latter taking over to deal with banditry. 
- Conclusion: the disappearance of the village elders led to numerous proposals for new organizations of village life from the JJ reign on, but Matsumoto suggests that the top-down structures may have been less important than reportedly "natural" "gatherings" 会 of 100-300 households, potentially a "relatively natural, free organization," but he doesn't go into detail. 

赋，役制度的变革 - 小山正明 1992
- Starts by defining the Mingmo-Qingchu fiscal system as the transitional phase between the Tang-Song (两税) system and the post-revolutionary system; the Single Whip inaugurating the system which became the target of land reform under the Peoples' Republic. 
- Begins his narrative of silverization with Zhou Chen in Jiangnan; Zhou was attempting to deal with the loss of tax funds from the guantian. 
	- Mainly cites earlier Japanese scholarship, e.g. 清水泰次 。 
- 十段法: core principle was to levy a fixed silver fee on ding and tian as registered in the huangce; first reform which did not rely on hu as basic unit of account, perhaps a sign of the chaos in household regsitration. The ding and tian here were 均等化 - i.e. silver levies ignored the old household wealth rankings.
	- Included both the 銀差 and 力差, combining them into a single payment. Clearly prefigures Single Whip unification.
	- One aim of ignoring the hu registrations was to get around the massive extension of tax-exempt status to degree-holders, as more and more hu were claiming such status, combined with splitting households to maximize corvee obligations.
- Single Whip: Dates earliest mention of Single Whip to a 1530 memorial by Gui E, describing debates within the 戶部. The principle was to add up all the 丁 + 稅 for the whole zhou/xian, subtract exempt 丁, then divide to find the total silver levy for the whole unit. In Jiajing 10 傅漢臣 called this the 一條鞭法 -- this was thus under discussion by the hubu in the 1530s. 
	- Differed from the 十段 in collating levies at the level of the fu or province, and in collecting annually rather than decennially. 
		- NB: Recall the Huizhou silk tax dispute; increasing push to "equalize" at higher levels of organization, perhaps a reflection of growing "regional" consciousness among gentry who, by now, have transcended their "village" hierarchies and formed a cross-prefectural or -provincial network?
	- The grain tax was progressively "unified" throughout the Jiajing years, but the corvee only came at the end of Jiajing, w/ Pang in Zhejiang combining 均平銀 + 均徭銀 at the 州縣 level, and 劉光濟 in Jiangxi also adding the 驛傳 and 民狀 to this.
- Conclusion: Sees the need to work around 鄉紳 tax privileges as running through all fiscal reforms from the 十段 on, as they routed around the 戶. Final result was to make the land itself the target and the village the agent of taxation, cutting out intermediary lijia. 
	- On one hand this represents the recovery of peasant agriculture and the strengthening of village bonds; on the other it reflects the tendency to do away with Hongwu's complex system of statuses in favor of a simple state-subject binary, where "subjects" were basically conceived of as farming landholders and their dependents. 
		- Note: per Brook, we could also see this as abdication of the lowest level of administrative hierarchy and "retreat" to a more manageable scale, in face of inadequate gov. resources to administer the whole population. 
	- Ultimately, the removal of tax collection to the 州縣 level put considerable power in the hands of 胥吏, who themselves were tightly connected to the 鄉紳 -- the gentry thus emerged from the long process of reform with yet another avenue to further their dominance of local societies... 


谷口規矩雄 - 日本的明代徭役研究
- Literature review of pre- and post-war Japanese scholarship on Ming corvee. 
- With the commutation of labor services, the distinction between 正管戶 and 帶管戶, among other service-based distinctions, tended to disappear; what would be the meaning of this for local village status relations? 57
- Taniguchi's question: how stable were the privileges conferred by liangzhang/li/jiazhang status really？How did the status of these positions change as the content of their responsibilities shifted? Could lijia status really compare to lineage and education based status hierarchies in local importance?
- Miscellaneous Service
	- The zayi varied greatly, but in general it was wealth-based, levied on estimates of men and land. The junyao reform was passed in order to (theoretically) make these assessments more consistent. Main effect was to set fixed terms and amounts on the zayi (which was levied decennially, five years apart from the zhengyi). Most important point is that land was the main metric for junyao levies, unlike for zhengyi. 
	- The classic work in Japanese on the junyao is 山根幸夫, "十五，十六世紀中國," 1966. 
		- Yamane's work was the first to show that the junyao intially was not silver-based (despite what the Ming Shi Shihuozhi says), but was rather commuted to silver by various Hongzhi/Zhengde reforms, which largely aimed to lessen the burden of the zayi. 
	- In Huabei, silver was collected on the basis of household rank in the nine-step system as a part of the junyao levies under the name 聽差 from at least the Chenghua reign. 64
	- The 十段法 as a response to the perceived inequalities opened up by the silverization of the junyi, which imposed payments on households based on census records which might be highly outdated, in particular due to the power of elites to hide their landholdings; reforming administrators responded by simply setting a levy for the full li and splitting it into equal tenths, to be paid be each jia as they wished. 
	- Most thorough study of the Shiduan fa came in the two-part article by Oyama Masaaki. (Included in this volume.)
		- For Oyama, the shiduan fa was a response to the failure of mid-Ming lijia registers to create the equality between taxpaying households which was their original intention, largely because of the aggrandizement of wealthy households under single hu; the solution was thus to find ways to 析戶. However, when gentry households were split up, it created more (tax-free) gentry households, which could go on to concentrate land around their new nuclei. The shiduan fa (which imposed payments without denominating by hu at all) was one way to cut this knot. 
	- Early Japanese scholarship on the Single Whip tended to focus on Huabei, esp. Shandong (in part due to Naito Konan's discovery of the 山東經會錄). An early exception was 岩見宏's 江西一條鞭法雜考, which focused on the Xunfu 劉光濟's enactment of the Single Whip in Jiangxi, particularly his relationship with Xu Jie. Very similar to my project in the dissertation -- must read. 
		- "一直以來，一條鞭法是各地方官應對當地實情，個別創立推行的。但是，江西的情況稍稍有些不同，是領會了中央意向之後再推行改革的。" 72
