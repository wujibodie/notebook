The idea of a "Qingyuan School" around Cai Qing is belied by the fact that Lin Xiyuan and Zhang Yue never actually met Cai; Chen Chen did, but probably only briefly early in his life. Further, of the three, only Lin wrote commemorative essays for Cai Qing: "Lin Xiyuan was the only person in the trio to have written commemorative essays for Cai Qing. He penned a detailed biography of Cai Qing which became the authoritative reference for Cai’s life. He was also active in the publication and dissemination of Cai Qing’s monumental works – Sishu mengyin and Yijing mengyin (Commentary guide for youth on the Four Books and Classic of Changes) – for both of which he wrote prefaces. Lin was the most vigorously sectarian scholar of the Quanzhou scholars. " 102
- Generally suggests that the idea of a coherent "Qingyuan School" was an invention of later scholars, primarily Li Qingfu in his Minxue yuanyuan kao.
